When should you rebuild a website?

Build a new website

For a business that needs a different product structure, multilingual content or a catalog with many connected pages.

Improve an existing website

For websites that already attract customers but are difficult to scale, contain inconsistent data or make technical SEO changes unnecessarily costly.

Migrate without losing valuable pages

Before changing platforms, I map old and new URLs, content, redirects, analytics and metadata, then verify the release.

What the project can include

The scope depends on the business. I plan page structures, source data and publishing rules before implementation.

  • Search-informed architecture: products, services, industries, common buyer questions, comparisons, cases and links between them.
  • Content models: reusable fields and templates for products, tours, services, people, prices and other business entities.
  • SEO and GEO: crawlable HTML, titles, descriptions, canonical tags, hreflang, sitemaps, Schema.org and verifiable source references.
  • Usability: responsive layouts, navigation, catalog search, enquiry forms and clear commercial information.
  • Quality checks: links, data integrity, forms, images, mobile layouts, performance and deployment procedures.
  • Handover: source files, documentation, publishing instructions and a plan for future improvement.

How site architecture supports GEO

AI services need accessible, consistent facts about what a business sells, who a product serves, how it is priced and what evidence supports its claims. I therefore focus on data structure, usable content and links between relevant pages.

Moving platforms or adding Schema.org cannot guarantee a recommendation from ChatGPT or any other AI service. GEO outcomes must be measured separately. Related work includes our e-commerce catalog methodology and GEO for online retailers.

Website development questions

Do I need a new website for GEO?+
Not necessarily. If your current platform supports useful pages, editable metadata and reliable data, targeted improvements may be enough. I first review the architecture.
Can existing URLs survive a platform migration?+
Often, yes. I map existing addresses before launch, preserve important URLs where practical and set up redirects when paths need to change. Canonical tags and sitemaps are checked too.
Will AI services recommend my business once the site is built?+
There is no such guarantee. A well-structured website makes business facts easier to discover, but recommendations also depend on external sources, competitors and the user's questions.
Can you work on an online store or travel website?+
Yes. Online retailers need useful product and comparison data; travel sites need linked tours, destinations and excursions. The data model is specific to the project.
How can I get a quote?+
A quotation is based on the current website, project objectives, planned sections and required integrations. I review these before proposing a scope of work.
