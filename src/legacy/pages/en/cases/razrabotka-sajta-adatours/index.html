Travel website development case

Developing AdaTours.ru with Astro: tours, destinations, excursions and GEO-ready pages

Alexey YakovlevPublished: October 26, 2026

AdaTours.ru uses Astro with connected tour, country, destination and excursion models, static publishing and technical SEO/GEO checks.

A tour operator website must support inspiration and precise product information: itinerary, places, excursions, daily program and price conditions. Ada Tours uses a connected content model instead of independent copies of overlapping tour pages.

Travel catalog requirements

Ada Tours works with travel in Brazil and Latin America, including individual and group tours and DMC/B2B services. The same country, place or excursion can appear across different itineraries, so their information should be linked and checked for consistency.

Additional user journeys include VIP, MICE, business delegations, themes and multi-country routes.

Astro and eight content collections

The site uses Astro static generation and Astro Content Collections. Its source schema defines countries, destinations, tours, excursions, themes, cases, people and articles. Tour records can include countries, places, duration, day-by-day itinerary, inclusions and structured pricing fields.

Separate values for price, currency, pricing basis and explanatory notes help avoid confusing a per-person rate with a total tour price.

Routes, relationships and site search

The source tree contains /country/, /interests/, /multi-country/, /dmc/, /vip/ and other route families. Automated checks validate links between related entities and multi-country navigation.

Pagefind builds a static search index. The public starting point is AdaTours.ru.

SEO/GEO and release verification

Astro generates static HTML and a sitemap excluding designated archived or redirected routes. The build scripts contain metadata, Schema.org, site-link, multi-country menu, media and social-preview checks.

The content model supports RU, EN and CN locale values. This is not proof that every itinerary is already public in all three languages. Current tour counts, performance scores and conversions require separate measurements.

My role and evidence limits

I contribute website architecture, the SEO/GEO model, technical requirements, content workflows and acceptance checks. Ada Tours must confirm the actual tour programs, rates and client commitments. Implementation also uses automation, so I do not claim sole manual authorship of the codebase.

This is a website development case. The separate Ada Tours GEO promotion case deals with BMR. The entire AI visibility increase cannot be attributed to the site alone.

Frequently asked questions

Why separate destinations and excursions from tour records?

A place or excursion can relate to several itineraries while retaining consistent data and links.

Does the build check technical SEO?

Yes. Structured data, metadata, routes and linking have dedicated checks.

Does this case prove a sales increase?

No. Business impact needs separate evidence from leads, conversions and attribution.
