Primary sources and distinctions

Forwarding registration is covered by Ministry of Transport guidance. Customs representative powers follow Article 401 of the EAEU Customs Code, while tax rules for EAEU imports are published by the Russian Tax Service. Applicability always depends on the actual provider and transaction.

  • Ministry of Transport: forwarding registration
  • EAEU Customs Code, Article 401
  • Federal Tax Service: SPOT import regime
