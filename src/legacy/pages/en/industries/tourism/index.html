GEO for tour operators and destination management companies

Alexey YakovlevUpdated: October 20, 2026

I help travel companies become relevant AI recommendations for specific destinations, trip formats and agency or traveler needs.

A destination management company competes on different questions from a single tour seller. An advisor may ask for a Brazil DMC, a private Patagonia program, an Antarctic cruise or an organized business delegation. Each audience and language uses a different shortlist of providers and sources.

Define travel intent by destination, buyer and service

I separate the country, traveler segment and operational complexity of the request. Different questions require different evidence and will often reveal different competitors.

  • A Brazil destination management company for an overseas travel agency.
  • A private Patagonia trip with local guides and transfers.
  • A business delegation with meetings, interpreters and ground support.
  • A multi-country Latin America itinerary with internal flights and coordination.

Agency buyers need proof of partner operations; private travelers care about the scope and flexibility of service. A sightseeing article is not a substitute for a page demonstrating who can organize the complete trip.

Why markets and languages need separate measurements

Russian and English prompts differ in wording, distribution and competitive context. Translating a service page is valuable, but each market needs locally relevant supporting material and a comparable measurement series.

Ada Tours: a fixed-matrix case

For Ada Tours, the published Russian-market test used 40 commercial prompts across 5 AI interfaces, producing 200 answers per measurement. Over 2 weeks, the proportion of answers explicitly mentioning the company rose from 10% to 35%.

How I approach DMC GEO

  1. Establish commercial prompt groups by country, buyer and language.
  2. Verify that travel and B2B pages match the company's actual capacity.
  3. Review partner profiles, media, relevant reviews and third-party evidence.
  4. Repeat the same measurements and assess both recommendations and qualified inquiries.

For complex B2B travel, proving operational fit is generally more useful than merely publishing more generic destination pages.

Frequently asked questions

How is GEO for a DMC different from promoting an excursion?+
A DMC often sells multi-service trip management, agency partnerships, logistics and group support. The buying prompts and evidence differ from those for a standalone excursion.
Can Russian and English markets share one BMR metric?+
Separate series are more informative because prompts, target customers, cited sources and competing operators may differ.
Why not compare BMR after adding more destinations?+
Changing the destination mix changes the tested questions and denominator. Preserve the original prompt set to measure a comparable trend.
What helps a travel company earn AI recommendations?+
Verifiable service capabilities, realistic itineraries, operational examples and relevant independent sources. Claims must match the services the company actually provides.
