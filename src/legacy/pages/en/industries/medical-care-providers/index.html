How healthcare GEO is measured

Comparable patient decision questions are checked against official provider information, then repeated using the same measurement method.

  • Prompts grouped by specialty, clinician, test, age, address and appointment
  • Verified licensed work, clinical staff, branch details and real prices
  • BMR changes, accuracy of recommendations and protection of medical confidentiality

This is a sector GEO methodology, not a completed healthcare client case. GAEO does not provide medical diagnosis or promise treatment outcomes. Shared pricing and BMR contract terms remain in force.
