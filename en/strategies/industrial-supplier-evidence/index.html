Industrial B2B GEO: how AI chooses steel structure suppliers

Alexey YakovlevPublished: October 23, 2026Updated: October 26, 2026Industry methodology

A useful industrial supplier recommendation requires a match between the procurement task and the contractor's actual scope: documentation, product type, manufacturing work and supporting evidence.

An industrial buyer may have no preferred steel structure manufacturer. Their question describes the building, drawings, fabrication scope and delivery constraints. An AI assistant can produce a shortlist, but insufficient evidence may cause it to confuse the engineering consultant, fabricator and installation contractor.

The GAEO commercial page for steel structure GEO explains what research and website work I propose. This methodology covers evidence and repeatable verification.

1. Separate KM drawings, KMD details, fabrication and erection

GOST 21.502-2016 regulates the composition and execution of KM structural working drawings. Its stated scope excludes KMD fabrication detail drawings. This matters in supplier selection because the design organization, detailer and fabricator may be separate businesses.

GOST 23118-2019 sets general technical specifications for its stated class of steel building structures, including relevant fabrication and quality matters. It does not cover every steel item or all forms of process equipment; the document lists exclusions.

SP 16.13330.2017 concerns steel structural design. Engineering practitioners, rather than a GEO consultant, establish how the applicable requirements and amendments apply to a particular project.

2. Build intent matrices from real procurement stages

Do not aggregate requests for engineering, KM/KMD detailing, fabrication and on-site erection into one uniform supplier-recommendation metric. The providers and evidence are different even when the final building is similar.

3. Connect the manufacturer's name to actual project evidence

A strong supplier page gives an accurate company identity, confirmed services, structural types and properly described industrial references. For each project, clarify the firm's actual role: detailing, fabrication, delivery or installation. A photograph of a completed warehouse does not by itself establish who manufactured its steel frame.

Technical proof can include permitted data on component types, dimensions, documented fabrication and quality procedures, subject to review. Private customer drawings, confidential commercial terms and unverified volume claims should not be made public to improve AI visibility.

4. What an anonymized industrial diagnostic can establish

In a preliminary GAEO review dated October 8, 2026, four procurement intents were tested across four AI interfaces, producing 16 responses. Manual inspection confirmed one relevant supplier mention, which an automatic brand match had initially missed due to a spelling variant. The observed value 1/16 (6.25%) belongs to one business and one initial sample.

Queries relating to KM/KMD, industrial applications and warehouses yielded different competitive sets. The material reviewed does not confirm completed optimization measures or a subsequent comparable uplift. These are diagnostic observations and work-planning inputs, not a client success case.

5. Website SEO and technical evidence must support the real scope

Distinct, indexable service and project pages should explain genuine specialties. A technical audit reviews canonical URLs, content accessibility, meaningful headings and related project links. Suspected duplication must be confirmed by inspecting actual pages and redirects before it is treated as a proven defect.

Organization, Service, Product or Article markup can reflect real entities and content. Google's official structured-data guidance calls for markup that matches visible page content. Do not mark up equipment or services a factory does not actually provide.

6. Verify technical facts using appropriate sources

Engineering standards define document or product requirements; they do not rank manufacturers. Qualification also requires the supplier's own accurate records, permitted project descriptions, scope of works and, where appropriate, independent technical publications. A product or batch quality record should be relevant to the actual order being assessed.

External editorial material is valuable when it identifies a verifiable supplier capability. Paid distribution should not be presented as independent endorsement. Industry claims must be approved by competent technical reviewers.

7. Measure mentions, links and independent recommendations separately

Record the exact prompt, selected AI provider, date, full text and sources. Three distinct signals are a company mention, a link to its site and an explicit recommendation. Manually review name variants, transliterations and similar supplier names. Missing answers should not count as negative recommendations.

A preliminary panel can be collected manually without purchasing monitoring services. Repeat comparable prompts and model interfaces for later comparisons, and evaluate qualified leads through appropriate business data. GAEO's BMR and Share of Voice methodology describes the general measurement framework.

Primary engineering references and limitations

GOST 21.502-2016 covers KM drawings. GOST 23118-2019 covers specified steel building structures, subject to its exclusions. SP 16.13330.2017 concerns structural design. For a particular project, an engineering team must confirm applicability and current amendments.

Frequently asked questions

Why split KM, KMD and manufacturing prompts?+

KM structural working drawings and KMD fabrication details represent different tasks. GOST 21.502-2016 sets requirements for KM and explicitly excludes KMD drawings. Engineering firms, detailers and manufacturers may be distinct suppliers; their visibility must be assessed separately.

Which official sources are relevant to steel building structures?+

GOST 21.502-2016 addresses KM documentation; GOST 23118-2019 covers general technical requirements for its defined category of steel building structures; SP 16.13330.2017 deals with steel structural design. Verify the applicable version, amendments and product scope for a real project.

May client drawings and detailed specifications be published?+

Only where contractual terms, confidentiality and intellectual property rights permit it. Industrial experience can often be described by project category, supplier role and confirmed characteristics without exposing client documents.

How should a manufacturer prove production capacity and project volume?+

Use appropriate production records, verifiable public information and validation by an authorized company representative. Numbers shown in marketing materials do not automatically establish capacity or ownership of every cited project.

How can a small industrial GEO baseline be measured?+

Use an explicit set of unbranded procurement questions across selected AI systems. Preserve complete answers, dates and citations. Count independent recommendations separately from mentions and links, and manually check name variants.

Are paid monitoring tools needed for the first analysis?+

No. A useful preliminary matrix can be collected manually. Automation becomes relevant when more prompts, sources or repeated periods justify it. Technical standards themselves do not define AI recommendation eligibility.
