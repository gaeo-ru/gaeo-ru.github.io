Why I collect external publications in one place

My own website can describe my specialization, services, methodology and results in detail. It is still a source I control myself.

That is why independent pages also matter for GEO: publications on industry platforms, interviews, case studies, rankings, professional profiles and reviews.

The monitoring data from my own GEO project shows this in practice. At different times, ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Yandex Alice, Perplexity and other AI platforms have used GAEO.ru, Workspace, Sostav, RBC Companies, Yandex Services and other sources.

The more consistent independent documents confirm the same professional entity, the more reliably an AI system can identify the specialist and consider them for recommendations.
