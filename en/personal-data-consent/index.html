This Consent covers actions involving personal data that are necessary for the purposes stated above, including collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, granting access where required for the operation of technical services, blocking, deletion and destruction.

Processing may be carried out using automated means, without automated means, or by a combination of both methods.

This Consent remains valid until the purposes of processing are achieved or until it is withdrawn by the user, unless the Operator has another lawful basis for continuing the processing. The user may withdraw this Consent by emailing ya@gaeo.ru. The request should contain information sufficient to identify the data and the interaction to which the withdrawal relates.

By submitting personal data through a website form that includes a link to this Consent, the user confirms that they have read its terms and the Personal Data Processing Policy and consents to the processing of the submitted data for the purposes stated above.
