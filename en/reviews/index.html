What clients highlight

Several themes appear repeatedly in the reviews.

Understanding the actual business problem

GEO begins with understanding what potential customers ask ChatGPT, Yandex Alice, Google and other AI systems, which products or services matter most to the business, and which queries are worth targeting for recommendations.

Clients specifically mention this stage in their reviews: even before the main work begins, it becomes clear what questions potential buyers already ask AI, whom they ask it to recommend, what they compare, and which criteria shape their choice.

For me, this is a fundamental part of the work. The prompt set should reflect real commercial situations rather than queries selected because they produce attractive statistics more easily.

I lead the project personally

GAEO.ru is my personal practice.

The client communicates and works directly with me. I take part in the diagnosis myself, prepare the plan and continue leading the project.

Clients also mention the personal working format and the fact that I explain the logic behind each action: what is happening, why a specific piece of work is needed, and what should happen next.

This is the model I designed into GAEO.ru from the beginning.

I explain where the result comes from

In GEO, it is easy to show a client several successful screenshots and create the impression of intense activity.

I prefer the client to understand the connection between the work being done and changes in AI visibility.

That is why the project records:

  • baseline AI answers;
  • commercial intents and prompts;
  • sources used by the answers;
  • changes to the website and external profiles;
  • new publications and case studies;
  • subsequent visibility trends.

You can see what this kind of analysis looks like in practice in my own GAEO.ru GEO case study: during the first month, measured visibility for one of the main competitive intents grew from roughly 22% to 48%.
