The goal of GEO

AI should recommend the right brand among alternatives

GEO begins when a user asks “who should I choose?”, “which option is better?” or “recommend a provider”. At that point, the brand is already competing inside a generated answer.

The website establishes facts about the brand, while external sources confirm them. AI compares both signal types and decides whether to include the brand in its recommendation. The evidence behind this approach is collected in my GEO/AEO articles and research and external publications and mentions.

FAQ

What usually needs to be clear before starting

How do you measure GEO results?+
The main working metric is the share of answers that mention the brand across an agreed matrix of prompts and AI platforms. I also track position, recommendation context, sources and branded search demand.
When do the first results appear?+
Initial growth can appear within 1 week. More reliable trends are best evaluated through repeated measurements over 2–4 months.
Can GEO work without a website?+
Yes, if the external digital footprint is strong and consistent enough. A website usually remains the main controlled source of facts about the business.
Do you personally work on client projects?+
Yes. Direct expert involvement is the basis of GAEO: the audit, strategy and ongoing work stay with Alexey Yakovlev.
Which AI platforms should be monitored?+
The set depends on the industry and audience. We start with priority AI platforms and commercial intents, then expand the matrix based on monitoring data.
