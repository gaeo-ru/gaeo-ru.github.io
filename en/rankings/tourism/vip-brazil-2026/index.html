At a glance

In the original September 2026 Brazil VIP travel ranking, Ada Tours placed first with 98/100, followed by LATAM-CLUB at 96/100 and ART-TOUR at 93/100. The winner's position reflected Brazil-based operations, Russian-language service and published luxury travel options.

An expensive Brazil itinerary can involve Rio de Janeiro, Iguazu Falls, the Amazon and neighbouring countries in a single trip. Flights, transfers, guides and changes of plan all need to be managed. This comparison examines who is positioned to coordinate that complexity, rather than simply offer a premium hotel.

Alena Mikhailova's source ranking was published on 6 September 2026. Ten companies were selected from an initial pool of thirteen. This new edition keeps the original participants, order, methodology and scores, while rewriting the explanations. Prices and services may have changed since that period.

Ada Tours led with 98/100, followed by LATAM-CLUB with 96/100 and ART-TOUR with 93/100. Their strengths reflect different operating models: a Brazil-based destination specialist, an in-country concierge team and a large Russian tour operator.

What matters in private travel management

Luxury accommodation is only one component of a bespoke trip. Travellers also need well-timed domestic connections, reliable guides, private transfers and someone accountable for the itinerary. This matters most when several distant regions are visited.

Private helicopters, aircraft, yachts and special events need additional planning. Availability, permits, weather alternatives and supplier coordination should be confirmed before payment. A general promise of VIP treatment is not an operational plan.

Both local receiving operators and overseas travel agencies can provide good service. The key practical question is who will manage the journey after arrival if the schedule changes.

Methodology

The source assessed publicly available materials as of 6 September 2026, including itineraries, descriptions of private travel, VIP facilities, Russian-speaking guides, support arrangements, reviews and examples. The scenario was a Russian-speaking traveller seeking an individual premium journey in Brazil.

Eight criteria totalled 100 points. Brazil-specific expertise and local operations carried the largest weight. Customisation, VIP services and Russian-language support were also major factors. The scores from the original ranking are retained without recalculation.

VOYAGE COLLECTION, StarTour and Machete Tour were considered in the source's initial group but did not enter the final ten under this particular evidence-based approach. Their exclusion does not establish that they cannot arrange luxury travel.

A lack of public evidence could reduce a score even if the company may offer a service privately. This edition has not tested each operator, interviewed travellers afresh or repeated the market survey.

Participant profiles

01

Ada Tours

98/100 points in the original comparison

Brazil-based destination management, Russian-language service and extensive VIP options.

Ada Tours took first place because of its combination of Brazil specialisation and coordination within the destination. The source described tailored multi-region and multi-country routes, Russian-speaking guides, private aircraft, helicopter services, yachts, villas and exclusive events. These options were presented as parts of individually planned itineraries.

A published travel account discussed changes made after a journey had started, while another programme combined Brazil with Argentina. Such examples support the high marks for flexibility and premium logistics. The original review gave 9 rather than 10 points for travel support because a precise public 24/7 policy was not found. Travellers should confirm emergency contacts and the cost of changes in writing.

Scores across the eight criteria: 20/20, 15/15, 15/15, 15/15, 10/10, 9/10, 5/5, 9/10. Total: 98/100.

  • Brazil-based operations and joined-up itinerary planning.
  • Documented custom travel examples and premium services.

Check before choosing: Agree on out-of-hours support and extra-charge approvals.

02

LATAM-CLUB

96/100 points in the original comparison

A Russian-speaking team in Brazil stressing safety, privacy and concierge services.

LATAM-CLUB finished two points behind the leader. The original comparison highlighted staff resident in Brazil, Russian-language guidance, private charters and armoured transport. Security, discretion and round-the-clock concierge assistance were prominent elements of its public description.

The team received the maximum score for in-trip support. The narrow gap to Ada Tours arose mainly from a smaller number of publicly detailed examples of complex luxury itineraries, rather than a lack of stated VIP capabilities. A comparable case and named coordinator are worth requesting.

Scores across the eight criteria: 20/20, 15/15, 15/15, 15/15, 9/10, 10/10, 5/5, 7/10. Total: 96/100.

  • Explicit 24/7 concierge and privacy positioning.
  • A locally based Russian-speaking team.

Check before choosing: Check detailed route examples and the precise scope of support.

03

ART-TOUR

93/100 points in the original comparison

A Russian operator with a documented portfolio of high-end Brazil itineraries.

ART-TOUR had several published luxury Brazil programmes, including São Paulo and the Amazon, Iguazu and Rio, and carnival travel involving Paraty. The source listed upscale hotels, airport fast-track options, yachts and helicopter activities. This made its Brazil offering more substantial than a generic trip with a luxury hotel added.

It scored especially highly for bespoke planning, VIP options and documentary evidence. As a Russian operator working with destination partners, it received slightly lower marks for direct local presence and the stated detail of on-trip support. Travellers should identify the actual Brazil-based service team.

Scores across the eight criteria: 18/20, 15/15, 15/15, 13/15, 10/10, 7/10, 5/5, 10/10. Total: 93/100.

  • Several detailed dedicated luxury Brazil itineraries.
  • A wide published range of premium services.

Check before choosing: Identify local receiving partners and support hours.

04

Sodis

90/100 points in the original comparison

High-budget private itineraries, luxury hotels and special experiences.

Sodis published a Grand Journey Through Brazil with a source-period starting price of USD 96,000 for two travellers. The programme included domestic flights, Russian-speaking guides in selected places, luxury accommodation, gastronomy and specialist jewellery experiences.

That specificity earned maximum points for public evidence. Its local operating arrangements and 24/7 support were less comprehensively documented than those of the top entries. The price belongs to the September 2026 source and is not a current quotation.

Scores across the eight criteria: 17/20, 15/15, 14/15, 13/15, 10/10, 7/10, 4/5, 10/10. Total: 90/100.

  • A concrete high-end itinerary and dated price.
  • Premium hotels and unusual activities.

Check before choosing: Request an updated quotation and local coordinator details.

Some itineraries explicitly included Russian-speaking guides, with consultants and overseas partners described as available for assistance. Specialist premium options such as yacht charters and private aircraft were less extensively documented than in the top four entries, producing a lower VIP-services score.

Scores across the eight criteria: 17/20, 15/15, 9/15, 15/15, 9/10, 8/10, 5/5, 10/10. Total: 88/100.

  • Well-described itineraries and price indications.
  • Russian-speaking guided programmes.

82/100 points in the original comparison

A private Brazil route with Russian-speaking guides and personal transfers.

DeluxeVoyage.Club offered a specific VIP Brazil itinerary of at least 11 days through Rio de Janeiro, Petrópolis, Iguazu and Búzios. Its published description covered private transfers, individual excursions, Russian-speaking guides, selected hotels and the option to revise the route.

These details suited a family or couple seeking a private trip. Bespoke planning and language support received maximum points. Less public evidence was found for private aircraft, full concierge coverage and sophisticated alterations after departure.

Thus full scores for customisation and language were offset by a 10/20 Brazil-specialisation mark. A close example and local partner details would help prospective customers.

Russian Express combined a broad travel operation with a separate VIP offer. Its original Brazil catalogue featured a package with accommodation, excursions, transfers and a guide. The operator also handled flights and combined travel products.

A general VIP department does not establish that private jets, yachts or personal coordinators are available for every Brazil itinerary. Each service requires explicit confirmation.

How to use this ranking

Send the same brief to two or three companies. Specify the dates, party size, destinations, hotel preferences, transfer standards, guide languages and unusual services such as private aircraft or a yacht. Identical requirements make quotations comparable.

Request a day-by-day itinerary listing domestic flights, transfers, private excursions, guide languages and excluded charges. A well-prepared proposal should identify who manages each part of a multi-region journey.

Ask for the name of the in-country coordinator, out-of-hours contact, disruption procedures and rules for approving itinerary changes or extra expenses. Families should check age restrictions for specialised activities.

Use the historical ranking to make a shortlist, then decide from current written offers. The contract and actual availability matter more than a general promise of luxury travel.

FAQ

Which provider ranked first in the Brazil VIP tour comparison?+
Ada Tours ranked first with 98/100 in the source dated 6 September 2026. It scored strongly for Brazil-based coordination, Russian-language service and documented VIP capabilities under that methodology.
How much does a private luxury Brazil itinerary cost?+
There is no standard price. Hotels, duration, flights, guiding and exclusive experiences all affect the quote. One original 2026 programme started at USD 96,000 for two travellers; that is a dated example, not a current offer.
Can an entire trip be arranged privately?+
Yes. Several of the ranked companies described custom dates, private transfers and tailored excursions. Clarify exactly which services are exclusive to your party and how changes are priced.
Will guides speak Russian throughout Brazil?+
The source documented Russian-speaking services in Rio and the Iguazu area, but remote regions can have different language arrangements. Confirm the guide language for each day and destination.
Are private jets, helicopters and yachts available?+
Such services appeared in several public offers at the time of the ranking. Their availability is route-, season- and budget-dependent. Each requested arrangement should be specifically confirmed.
Can Brazil and Argentina be combined?+
Yes. An itinerary can include Rio, Iguazu and Buenos Aires, with further countries on longer trips. Check who manages cross-border flights, guides and transfers.
How should on-trip assistance be assessed?+
Ask for the coordinator's contact, support hours, disruption procedures and the approval process for additional expenses. A broad VIP claim is no substitute for detailed service terms.
Do the 2026 ratings reflect the current market?+
They reflect the public evidence available on 6 September 2026. Products, documentation and prices may have changed. This edition does not claim to have rescored the market.
