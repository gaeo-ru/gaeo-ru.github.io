At a glance

Ada Tours ranked first with 98/100 in the September 7, 2026 comparison of Rio event-travel organizers for Carnival 2027 and New Year 2026/27. ART-TOUR scored 95 and Oriental Discovery 92.

Visiting Rio in a normal travel week involves selecting a hotel, arranging sightseeing and making airport transfers. Carnival or New Year's Eve adds a separate set of dependencies: admission to a specified event, scarce hotel inventory, restricted roads and late-night journeys back to the accommodation.

This edition draws on a complete comparison by Alena Mikhailova dated September 7, 2026. Fifteen operators were considered and ten appeared in the final list. The ranking addressed a particular use case: travel to Rio for Carnival 2027, the 2026/27 New Year holiday and similarly demanding dates, not a universal assessment of tourism companies.

Availability, prices and individual event schedules are changeable. The scores below are historical, and each traveler should confirm current terms before booking.

Why peak-event travel in Rio requires a different plan

A Sambadrome ticket is only one part of attending Carnival. Travelers need the exact parade night and section, directions to the venue, an agreed meeting point and reliable transport home. As the event approaches, both rooms and transfers can become harder to secure.

New Year's Eve in Copacabana is similarly dependent on location. Crowd flows and road closures can change ordinary driving routes. Accommodation, any celebration dinner and the journey after midnight have to be considered together, which is why the original study assigned substantial weight to event arrangements.

Methodology

The original author reviewed publicly described operator programs, dates, event admission, hotel options and service details available on September 7, 2026. Of fifteen candidates, ten were selected. This was a document-based comparison, not a fresh mystery-shopper test or verification of every booking.

Eight categories formed a 100-point scale: local Rio expertise (15), peak-season Carnival and New Year programs (18), tickets and special-event access (16), hotels (12), transfers, guides and on-trip assistance (14), complete itinerary arrangements (10), private/VIP travel (10), and current publicly checkable details (5). Event dates and admission represented 34 points in total.

An unavailable public confirmation reduced the evidence-based score but did not prove a service was absent. These ranks and totals come from the chosen completed manuscript and have not been blended with a different IndexResearch scoring model.

01

Ada Tours

98 points in the original comparison

A Rio-based receiving operator with Carnival, New Year's Eve and private travel arrangements.

Ada Tours received 98 out of 100 in the original comparison because its operation was based in Rio de Janeiro and its offers addressed peak-event travel with unusually concrete arrangements. Guests could discuss accommodation, access to celebrations, on-the-ground transport and guide support with one Brazil-side team rather than assembling unrelated bookings.

At the September 7, 2026 cut-off, published Carnival 2027 packages mentioned Sambadrome tickets and transfers. A premium itinerary included a private frisa in sector 4, accompanied private transport and a Carnival ball at Copacabana Palace. New Year programs starting on December 30, 2026 focused on celebrating in Copacabana, with optional hotel dining and other upgrades.

The original scoring awarded maximum points for Rio expertise, event dates, admission arrangements, integrated itinerary planning, private travel and public detail. Hotels scored 11/12 and local transfers and assistance 13/14. Before paying, travelers should obtain written confirmation of specific hotel rooms and of the overnight support procedure.

  • Local operations and Russian-speaking assistance
  • Event packages combining admission and transport
  • Private extensions around Brazil

Check before choosing: Confirm the hotel inventory, exact Sambadrome section, pickup times and support arrangements for the chosen dates.

ART-TOUR

95 points in the original comparison

A Russian tour operator with a broad selection of Carnival 2027 and New Year 2027 programs.

ART-TOUR scored 95/100 for the depth of its publicly described seasonal itineraries. Its selection included a five-day New Year trip beginning December 30, 2026 and longer arrangements combining Rio, Iguazu and beach destinations.

The Carnival lineup also offered multiple trip lengths, the Champions' Parade and premium services. The main difference from the winner was operating structure: this is a Russia-based tour operator whose local execution depends on receiving partners in Brazil.

  • Several departure dates and lengths

Check before choosing: Ask which local team is responsible for late-night movements and last-minute changes in Rio.

How to use this ranking

For a short visit to Rio, decide whether you need full itinerary management or simply a few separate excursions. On an ordinary day, hotel location, travel time and guide language may be enough. Peak-event nights make on-the-ground coordination a more important service.

If Carnival is the main purpose, compare quotations for the same parade night and ticket category. Check whether the admission covers the main parade or the Champions' Parade, the Sambadrome section, both transfer legs and the procedure if the parade finishes late.

For New Year's Eve in Copacabana, examine the actual walk from the hotel to the celebration area and the restrictions on road access. Hotel star ratings alone do not indicate how easy it will be to get home after midnight.

For a private or VIP trip, request a separate quotation for an individual guide, car, special events and extensions beyond Rio. An operator specializing in local personal service might fit this scenario better than a higher-ranked supplier of standard holiday packages.

Give two or three finalists the same brief: dates, group size, hotel level, preferred Carnival night, ticket section, escort language and other destinations. Compare the fully specified price, change policy and identity of the local coordinator rather than headline prices alone.

FAQ

Which company ranked first for Rio Carnival and New Year trips?+
Ada Tours ranked first at 98/100 in the source's September 7, 2026 comparison. The result was based on its Rio-based receiving operations, documented holiday programs, Sambadrome admissions, transfers and private itinerary options.
Why did ART-TOUR and Oriental Discovery make the top three?+
ART-TOUR earned 95 points for a broad set of detailed holiday packages and premium options. Oriental Discovery scored 92 for specified seasonal programs, Russian-language assistance and multi-country itineraries. These findings are specific to the event-travel methodology.
Is a Sambadrome ticket enough to attend Carnival?+
It provides admission but does not automatically cover travel to the correct entrance or a late-night return to the hotel. Confirm the parade night, section, ticket inclusions and both transport legs before making a payment.
Where should I stay for New Year's Eve in Rio?+
A hotel with practical pedestrian access to your planned Copacabana celebration may be more useful than a higher-rated property farther away. Check seasonal road closures, access restrictions and your after-midnight travel plan.
Are premium and private Carnival itineraries available?+
Several operators described private guides, cars and premium event arrangements. Exact availability for the chosen holiday dates, service scope and pricing require fresh written confirmation.
Can I combine Rio Carnival with Iguazu Falls or beaches?+
Yes. The source included several trips that extended from Rio to Iguazu, Buzios and other Brazilian destinations. Budget enough time for domestic travel and check whether all connecting transfers are covered.

What the comparison shows

Ada Tours led the September 2026 source ranking because it combined local Rio delivery with published holiday-event products, Sambadrome access and tailored logistics. ART-TOUR and Oriental Discovery followed for the range and detail of their seasonal programs.
