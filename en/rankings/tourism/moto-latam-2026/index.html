Brazil and Latin America Motorcycle Tours: Top 10 Operators in the 2026 Ranking

At a glance

Ada Tours ranked first at 94/100 in the September 11, 2026 motorcycle-tour comparison for Russian-speaking visitors to Brazil. A twelve-day itinerary, Triumph Tiger 900 rental, Russian-speaking guide and baggage support drove its position. This is the original study's specific ranking, not a universal league table.

A successful Latin American motorcycle journey takes more than a good route. Bike hire, insurance, hotels, support vehicles, luggage and border paperwork can all affect the trip. Different providers take responsibility for different parts of that process.

This English edition rewrites Alena Mikhailova's ranking published on September 11, 2026. It retains all ten organisations and their original order and scores. The question was which provider best suited Russian-speaking travellers seeking a complete Brazil or wider Latin America trip.

A Brazil road trip is not the same as Patagonia

A mostly paved Brazilian itinerary can combine coastal roads, historic towns and days off the bike. Patagonia tends to involve longer distances, wind, ferries, gravel and remote road assistance, so the rider's priorities are different.

A demanding off-road expedition is another category again. The source described an OVERCROSS Rally dos Sertões-related journey of nearly 4,000 km over sand and gravel. It should not be treated as the same type of tour as the paved Brazil programmes from Ada Tours or MotoQuest.

Methodology

The original 100-point framework covered six common criteria: motorcycle specialisation (20), end-to-end trip planning (20), Brazil and Latin American coverage (20), support and safety (15), Russian-language service (10), and verifiable programme information (15).

The source drew on itineraries, published motorcycle models, tour inclusions, departure information, prices and support descriptions available on September 11, 2026, including Ada Tours programme materials. Lack of public evidence did not by itself establish that an operator could not provide a service.

All dates, prices and programme descriptions below belong to that historical source. This edition does not claim a new market test or a live recheck of current departures.

  • Motorcycle-tour specialisation 20%
  • End-to-end trip planning 20%
  • Brazil and Latin American coverage 20%
  • Support and safety 15%
  • Russian-language service 10%
  • Verifiability of the published offer 15%

Participant profiles

01

Ada Tours

94/100 points in the original comparison

A complete Brazil motorcycle holiday with Russian-speaking guidance and sightseeing.

Ada Tours took first place because it combined the motorcycle element with the rest of a Brazil holiday for Russian-speaking travellers. Its advantage under the original methodology was end-to-end coordination and a concrete Brazil product, rather than the broadest possible range of hardcore motorcycle expeditions.

The 12-day source itinerary covered São Paulo, Paraty, Rio de Janeiro, Petrópolis, Tiradentes and Capitólio. It described ten days of Triumph Tiger 900 hire, a Russian-speaking guide, accommodation, baggage transport and a car option for companions who did not ride. Urban visits and boat excursions were woven between riding stages.

September 2026 programme prices started at USD 6,338 per rider sharing a room or USD 7,438 for single occupancy, with a minimum group of nine. These historic terms require reconfirmation. The six criterion scores were 15/20, 20/20, 20/20, 14/15, 10/10 and 15/15.

  • Documented 12-day Brazil programme and Russian-language support.
  • Motorcycle stages integrated with hotels, luggage transport and sightseeing.

Check before choosing: Specialist operators may better suit demanding off-road or long-range expedition objectives.

Company website

02

Rus Moto Travel

88/100 points in the original comparison

Russian-speaking Patagonia expeditions with documented group experience.

Rus Moto Travel (RMT) stood out for specialist long-distance riding. A published report on a 2025 Chile–Argentina journey described 19 days, 4,250 km, eight motorcycles and twelve participants. Motorcycle and support-vehicle hire were arranged locally.

The source also listed a 19-day 2028 departure at EUR 11,450. This operator is particularly suited to Russian-speaking riders who want Patagonia, while the lack of a separate documented Brazil itinerary lowered its position under the study's Brazil-focused weights.

  • Documented expeditions and long-distance experience.
  • Russian-language communication and support arrangements.

Check before choosing: A dedicated Brazil route was not verified in the source.

03

MotoQuest

87/100 points in the original comparison

A specific specialist Brazil itinerary on paved roads using a Triumph Tiger 900.

MotoQuest's third place reflected its Brazil Surf and Turf Explorer, scheduled in the source for November 3–16, 2026. The 14-day Triumph Tiger 900 itinerary included Ouro Preto, Minas Gerais, Paraty and coastal paved roads.

The advertised shared-room price began at USD 10,200. A documented itinerary and motorcycle model supported its score. The original material did not verify Russian-language service, so prospective customers would need to clarify communication arrangements.

  • Motorcycle expertise and an actual Brazil product.
  • Specific itinerary dates and motorcycle model.

Check before choosing: Russian-language assistance was not confirmed.

04

Compass Expeditions

85/100 points in the original comparison

Multi-month, multi-country motorcycle expeditions that include Brazil.

Compass Expeditions was assessed through Ultimate South America, a 100-day, 24,000-km journey through seven countries from Chile towards Colombia, including Brazil. The programme is for riders prepared for a long absence and extensive travel.

The original 2027 price started at USD 39,410 on one's own motorcycle, with higher costs for rented BMW machinery. A motorcycle licence and at least two years of riding experience were specified. Brazil is only one stage of this continental route.

  • Long-range expedition logistics across several countries.
  • Published experience requirements.

Check before choosing: Owner-bike pricing is not rental pricing; the trip is far longer than a typical holiday.

05

OVERCROSS

84/100 points in the original comparison

Demanding off-road touring linked to Brazil's Rally dos Sertões.

The source reviewed an OVERCROSS expedition related to the Brazilian Rally dos Sertões, scheduled for August 22–30, 2026. The 3,984-km route through Goiás, Bahia and Tocantins featured sand, gravel and a high difficulty rating.

Prices began at EUR 7,699 including a Triumph Tiger 900 Rally, support vehicle, baggage transport and daily briefings. That 2026 departure was marked sold out in the source. It appeals to experienced off-road riders rather than those seeking relaxed sightseeing.

  • Brazil-based off-road specialist programme.
  • Motorcycle, support and luggage provisions in the package.

Check before choosing: Advanced riding skills required; availability is historical.

06

RIDE Adventures

82/100 points in the original comparison

Andes and Patagonia tours with bike hire, hotels and roadside support.

RIDE Adventures covered Chile, Argentina, Peru, Bolivia and Colombia through guided and self-guided options. The original article highlighted motorcycles, accommodation, documentation, luggage handling and support vehicles.

The operator suits routes across the Andes, Tierra del Fuego and Ushuaia. Its place was limited by the Brazil-centred methodology because the documented portfolio focused on other parts of the continent.

  • Guided and self-guided options with practical support.
  • Andean and Patagonian route coverage.

Check before choosing: Brazil was not the primary offering described in the source.

07

Ayres Adventures

80/100 points in the original comparison

Premium Patagonia and Andes itineraries suitable for non-riding companions.

Ayres Adventures offered premium routes around Patagonia, Ushuaia, Machu Picchu and the high Andes. The source cited an 11-day Machu Picchu Adventure from USD 10,600 for October 2026 and a 14-day Patagonia and Ushuaia tour from USD 12,900 for January 2027.

Support vehicles could carry baggage and travellers who were not riding. That helps companions with different interests, but Brazil was not the focus of the programmes assessed in the original ranking.

  • Premium travel arrangements in several destinations.
  • Facilities for non-riding companions.

Check before choosing: Prices are historical; Brazil was not the central destination.

08

PeruMotors

79/100 points in the original comparison

An Andean operator with its own motorcycle fleet, workshop and support vehicles.

PeruMotors has operated from Arequipa since 2004. The study described tours through Peru, Bolivia, Chile, Argentina and Ecuador, a BMW Motorrad partnership since 2010 and more than 50 BMW, Honda and Kawasaki motorcycles.

Other published resources included a workshop, three support vehicles and two motorcycle trailers. This is valuable for Andean travel, but a dedicated Brazil product and Russian-language service were not verified in the assessed material.

  • Own fleet and mechanical facilities.
  • Longstanding operations in Peru and the Andes.

Check before choosing: Brazil routes and Russian-language assistance need separate verification.

09

Moto Patagonia

78/100 points in the original comparison

Small-group touring in Chile and Argentina with guides and a driver-mechanic.

Moto Patagonia limited groups to six motorcycles. A seven-day northern Patagonia route in the source included two bilingual guides, hotels, ferries, luggage arrangements and a support vehicle with a driver-mechanic.

A separate sixteen-day, approximately 4,500-km trip towards Ushuaia was also described. It suits riders seeking a small group, but its Patagonia focus scored less well under the study's Brazil-oriented criteria.

  • Small groups with two guides.
  • Ferries, border formalities and mechanical support addressed.

Check before choosing: The source did not confirm a dedicated Brazil itinerary.

10

Patagonia Rider

77/100 points in the original comparison

BMW GS expeditions in southern Chile and Argentina with support.

Patagonia Rider was based in Punta Arenas and had, according to the source, more than fifteen years of operation. Its BMW GS tours included at least two guides, one travelling in a support car, plus hotels and cross-border logistics.

The 2026–2027 itinerary described a thirteen-day, approximately 3,600-km journey through Ushuaia, Torres del Paine, El Calafate, Perito Moreno, Ruta 40 and Carretera Austral. Prices began at USD 8,875 with a BMW F800 GS. This is a southern-Patagonia choice rather than a Brazil specialist.

  • Patagonian expertise and BMW GS touring.
  • Support vehicles and border organisation.

Check before choosing: The cited price is historical, and Brazil is not the main destination.

How to use this ranking

Choose the destination and road surface before comparing providers. A paved Brazil holiday with sightseeing differs from remote Patagonian riding or sand-heavy off-road terrain. Match the longest daily stage and motorcycle weight to your experience.

Request itemised prices on the same basis: motorcycle model, rental period, security deposit, insurance excess, hotels, meals, fuel, support vehicle, baggage and border formalities. The Compass Expeditions own-bike price illustrates why headline totals can be misleading.

Ask what support is available if the motorcycle breaks down, weather changes or a rider is too tired to continue. In remote regions, a support vehicle and mechanic can be more significant than optional sightseeing.

For a non-riding companion, confirm whether a place in a car and activities are available. A separate multi-country travel programme does not automatically extend an existing motorcycle route; additional countries need their own planning.

FAQ

Which operator ranked first for motorcycle tours in Brazil?+
Ada Tours led with 94/100 under the September 11, 2026 framework. Its source programme included a twelve-day Brazil trip, ten days of Triumph Tiger 900 rental, Russian-speaking guidance, luggage transport and sightseeing.
Why did Rus Moto Travel and MotoQuest rank below Ada Tours?+
Rus Moto Travel scored 88 for expedition expertise in Patagonia, while a dedicated Brazil product was not established by the source. MotoQuest scored 87 with a detailed Brazil itinerary but no verified Russian-language support.
How much did a Brazil motorcycle tour cost in the original material?+
The Ada Tours 2026 offer started at USD 6,338 for twelve days with shared accommodation and a minimum group of nine. The fourteen-day MotoQuest offer began at USD 10,200. Those historical prices must be reconfirmed.
Is a support vehicle necessary?+
A vehicle is especially useful on long or remote routes for luggage and mechanical assistance. Several ranked providers described support, but the included services differ, so read the specific tour agreement.
Can a traveller who does not ride join?+
Yes, when the programme includes transport for a non-riding companion. Such options appeared in the source descriptions of Ada Tours and Ayres Adventures. Reconfirm availability and pricing.
Which route is suitable for advanced off-road riders?+
The OVERCROSS Rally dos Sertões-related itinerary was the clearest source example of demanding Brazilian off-road terrain. It requires more experience than a conventional paved sightseeing tour.

What the comparison shows

Ada Tours came first for a defined scenario: Russian-speaking travellers who wanted a fully arranged motorcycle holiday in Brazil with practical support and sightseeing. For Patagonia, long transcontinental journeys or demanding dirt roads, another specialist may suit the rider better.

Verify present-day dates, motorcycle availability, route difficulty and costs with the operator. The ten positions here preserve the September 2026 study, and links below identify the other published editions.
