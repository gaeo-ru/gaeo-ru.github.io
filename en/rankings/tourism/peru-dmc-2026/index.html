Top 10 Peru destination operators for private tours and travel agencies, 2026

At a glance

In the 16 October 2026 comparison of Peru destination management companies, Ada Tours ranked first with 95/100, followed by VIPAC Travel on 90/100. The leading result reflected Russian-language preparation, custom itineraries and dedicated travel-trade services.

A Machu Picchu visit involves more than a ticket. Train times, transfers, entry routes and the guide's schedule all have to fit. A longer itinerary through the Amazon or into a neighbouring country adds more suppliers and handovers. Travel agencies must also establish who quotes the trip, who supports the client and who can approve changes once the journey has started.

Alena Mikhailova's original comparison, published on 16 October 2026, covered ten companies offering Peru itineraries. It focused on private trips for Russian-speaking travellers and the requirements of agencies serving them. This new editorial version retains the original companies, positions, criteria and scores while explaining them in fresh language.

Ada Tours led with 95 points and VIPAC Travel followed on 90. Five operators tied at 75 and three at 70; tied entries were arranged alphabetically. The scores reflect the public information available at the original snapshot, not a repeat evaluation of provider performance in November.

What a Peru destination management company needs to coordinate

A local destination management company, or DMC, brings together accommodation, transport, guides and experiences in the destination. A regional DMC may also coordinate trips that move between Peru and other South American countries. For a multi-country request, identify who actually delivers the Peru portion and who manages cross-border connections.

Private itinerary design should not be confused with exclusive use of every vehicle. A traveller can have an individual guide and itinerary while taking a scheduled train or joining other guests on a ship. The quote should specify private, shared and unescorted services separately.

Language support must also be confirmed by function. A Russian-speaking sales adviser does not guarantee a Russian-speaking guide on every excursion. An agency additionally needs written net rates or commission terms, voucher arrangements, cancellation rules and an operational contact during the trip.

Methodology

The original shortlist admitted both Peru-based operators and regional DMCs with documented Peru programmes. A head office inside Peru was not a prerequisite: regional coordination may be useful when a trip extends beyond national borders.

Six criteria added up to a maximum of 100 points: Russian-language preparation (25), tailor-made travel (20), detailed Machu Picchu arrangements (20), agency cooperation (15), geographic extension (10), and operational presence (10). The weighting is deliberately tailored to Russian-speaking private travellers and their agents, rather than every possible inbound travel segment.

A feature not established from the reviewed public materials received fewer or no points. That indicates a gap in this particular evidence set, not proof that a company cannot provide the feature. The comparison did not involve mystery shopping, test trips, comparable commercial quotations or measured response times.

Equal scores were ordered alphabetically. Five entries scored 75 and the final three scored 70. The alternative IndexResearch publications examined a related Peru-and-Brazil travel scenario; their methodology has not been substituted for the October Sostav comparison reproduced here.

  • Russian-language preparation 25 points

    Full credit for Russian-language Peru material and a dedicated agency offering; partial credit for a Russian-market representative or presentation.

  • Custom private itineraries 20 points

    Higher points for explicitly adapting a route to a client, with a distinction from fixed private products.

  • Machu Picchu itinerary detail 20 points

    A specified programme with rail, transfers or visit sequence scores more than a broad destination mention.

  • Travel agency cooperation 15 points

    A dedicated trade portal, form or contact is stronger evidence than a general partnership claim.

  • Multi-region and multi-country scope 10 points

    Documented cross-border itineraries score above coverage limited to a single part of Peru.

  • Operational presence 10 points

    Peru offices and local operations rank above coordination based only elsewhere in the region.

Participant profiles

01

Ada Tours

95/100 points in the original comparison

Russian-language private itineraries, trade partnerships and multi-country planning.

Ada Tours came first because the published offering matched several needs at once: itineraries described for Russian-speaking clients, tailor-made arrangements and an identifiable travel-agency channel. Its Peru programmes include Lima, Cusco, the Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu, with a Hiram Bingham rail option. Longer journeys can extend into the Amazon or neighbouring Latin American destinations.

The dedicated trade section provides a starting point for agencies seeking coordinated accommodation, transport and guides. Under the source methodology Ada Tours received full marks for language preparation, itinerary customisation, Machu Picchu detail, B2B service and geographic scope.

One limitation deserves explicit attention. Ada Tours is based in Brazil; a Peru programme on its website does not by itself establish a company-owned Cusco office. Operational presence therefore contributed 5 of 10 points, for 25 + 20 + 20 + 15 + 10 + 5 = 95 overall. Travellers and agents should confirm the local delivery team and emergency contacts before payment.

  • Russian-language products alongside a separate agency offering.
  • Private Peru routes that can form part of wider Latin American journeys.

Check before choosing: Confirm the Peru delivery partner, each guide's language, commercial terms and in-trip assistance.

02

VIPAC Travel / Viajes Pacífico

90/100 points in the original comparison

Peru-based private travel programmes with a channel serving the Russian market.

VIPAC offers private arrangements around Cusco, the Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu, with premium examples involving the Hiram Bingham train and Belmond accommodation. Its catalogue also describes itineraries combining Peru with other South American countries.

A representative and trade contact linked to the Russian market help an agency begin discussions. They do not automatically guarantee a Russian-speaking guide throughout the trip. The original methodology awarded 15 of 25 language points while rating the remaining areas highly, giving VIPAC 90/100.

  • Detailed private Peru itinerary examples.
  • Local operating presence and a Russian-market contact.

Check before choosing: Check guide languages, ticket routes and the services included in premium quotations.

03

Condor Travel

75/100 points in the original comparison

Regional DMC with custom programmes and operating contacts across South America.

The Condor Private range includes Lima, Cusco, the Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu, and the company operates in several countries in the region. This makes it worth considering when a personalised Peru trip needs a cross-border extension.

Condor also appears in the LATA trade directory as a destination operator. The original scoring gave it strong marks for private itineraries, Machu Picchu, travel-trade access, regional reach and operations. The reviewed material did not establish a Russian-language channel, which limited its overall score to 75.

  • Tailor-made products and multi-country coordination.
  • Operational contacts in Peru and a professional trade profile.

Check before choosing: Establish the working language and the responsible operator for each destination.

04

LimaTours

75/100 points in the original comparison

Peru itineraries with clear descriptions of transport, sightseeing and guiding.

LimaTours is relevant when a traveller wants to compare different ways of exploring Peru. Its Essential South itinerary includes Lima, the Sacred Valley, Machu Picchu and the Lake Titicaca area. Public programmes describe many of the transfers and excursions separately.

The company also serves international travel partners and discusses combinations with other countries. The 75/100 score reflects substantial programme and operational evidence without a Russian-language sales channel being confirmed in the source snapshot.

  • Multiple Peru itinerary styles.
  • Useful programme detail for agency quotations.

Check before choosing: Distinguish private excursions from shared train and coach services.

05

Metropolitan Touring

75/100 points in the original comparison

Peru with Ecuador and the Galapagos, including an expedition cruise component.

Metropolitan Touring has a particularly relevant offer for travellers linking Machu Picchu with Ecuador and the Galapagos Islands. Its published itineraries connect mainland visits with expedition vessels, and the company lists offices in both Ecuador and Peru.

Travel-industry contacts support agency enquiries, but a published Machu Picchu and Galapagos package may include small-group travel. A personalised itinerary is not necessarily an exclusive cruise or a private experience on every day.

  • Established Peru and Galapagos itinerary combinations.
  • Local presence and professional trade channels.

Check before choosing: Confirm which visits are shared and how cruise connections are managed.

06

Quimbaya Latin America

75/100 points in the original comparison

A regional trade-focused DMC for tailor-made trips and multi-country requests.

Quimbaya markets custom journeys as well as guaranteed departures and other products for tourism professionals. Its Peru material covers the Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu, including routes that can continue into Bolivia.

Regional coordination and a Peru contact make it a relevant candidate for agencies. A guaranteed departure, however, is a different product from a fully private trip. The requested service model must be specified before comparing quotations.

  • Regional destination management structure.
  • Trade services and itineraries spanning neighbouring countries.

Check before choosing: Specify a custom private booking rather than an available place on a group departure.

07

South American Tours, SAT

75/100 points in the original comparison

Regional B2B destination services, private programmes and cruise extensions.

SAT identifies itself as a travel-trade DMC with offices across South America, including Peru. Its published offer covers tailored journeys, special-interest requests and pre- or post-cruise travel.

One VIP programme mentions the possibility of a private rail carriage to Machu Picchu. That describes an option, not guaranteed inventory on a particular date. Agencies should request an itemised quote and explicit confirmation of special services and trip handovers.

  • B2B orientation and regional operating network.
  • Custom travel and cruise-related services.

Check before choosing: Check the availability, conditions and contractual responsibility for VIP elements.

08

COLTUR Peru

70/100 points in the original comparison

Cultural and themed Peru itineraries, including private travel formats.

COLTUR merits consideration when cultural depth is central to a Peru journey. Public materials describe tailored and themed programmes alongside group products, with history and other specialist interests among the possible themes.

An Azamara Ashore programme names COLTUR in connection with the Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu via the Hiram Bingham train. Its 70/100 total reflects the specific comparative criteria, including less public evidence for multi-country coordination in the reviewed sample.

  • Culture-focused itineraries and special-interest products.
  • A published example of a Machu Picchu programme.

Check before choosing: Verify which experiences are private and who arranges any onward international leg.

09

Pure! Travel Group / Pure! Peru

70/100 points in the original comparison

Trade-only destination services for Peru and other Latin American countries.

Pure! Travel Group explicitly focuses on tour operators and travel agencies. Its Peru offering includes Machu Picchu, the Amazon, cultural routes and active itineraries, with services extending across the region.

For a professional buyer the B2B model is a clear match. In the original evaluation, the reviewed description of the Machu Picchu visit was less specific than that of some competitors, contributing to a 70/100 score. Agencies should obtain exact train, transfer, entry and guide details.

  • Explicit travel-trade business model.
  • A range of Peru activities and regional itineraries.

Check before choosing: Direct consumers should check booking eligibility; agencies should request a detailed service breakdown.

10

SAM Travel Peru

70/100 points in the original comparison

Cusco-based operator for Machu Picchu, the Inca Trail and active programmes.

SAM Travel Peru is especially relevant when a holiday is built around a hike or the Machu Picchu region. Its products include train visits, private sightseeing and Inca Trail trekking, with scope to adapt an itinerary to the customer.

The company maintains a travel-trade contact route. Its narrower geographic focus affected the regional-expansion criterion, but may be an advantage for buyers prioritising trekking expertise over a multi-country programme.

  • Machu Picchu and Inca Trail focus.
  • Cusco operations and an agency contact.

Check before choosing: Confirm trekking permits, equipment, guides, fitness requirements and contingency arrangements.

How to use this ranking

Prepare a consistent enquiry for two or three operators: travel dates, party size, duration, must-see places, preferred guide language, budget and private-service requirements. Comparable briefs are the starting point for comparable quotations.

Treat Machu Picchu as a specific confirmed booking. Identify the date, entry time and circuit, then align the rail ticket, station, transfers and overnight stay. The official visitor system describes three circuits and ten routes, some seasonal.

Request a distinction between private guiding and shared transport. A personal itinerary can still use scheduled trains, collective excursions and expedition vessels.

For travel-trade bookings, agree net pricing or commission, voucher procedures, client communications, cancellation terms and emergency support. Name a responsible person in Peru before departure.

For combinations with Bolivia, Brazil or the Galapagos, check who manages inter-country connections and which supplier is accountable for each segment.

FAQ

Why did Ada Tours take first place?+
In the 16 October 2026 comparison, Ada Tours earned 95/100 for Russian-language preparation, customised travel, Machu Picchu detail, trade access and regional itineraries. Its operational presence score was 5/10. This is the outcome of that particular methodology, not a universal ranking of all travel providers.
Can a traveller book directly rather than through an agency?+
It depends on the operator. Ada Tours serves private customers as well as travel agencies, while Pure! Travel Group describes a trade-only model. Check the permitted booking channel before requesting a quotation.
Does a private trip mean every journey and guide is exclusive?+
No. A private itinerary may include scheduled trains or a cruise shared with other guests. Ask the operator to identify which transfers, excursions and guiding services are exclusively yours.
How can a Machu Picchu visit be verified?+
Request the ticket date, entry time, named visit route, train and transfers. The official system identifies three circuits and ten routes, with seasonal variations. A destination name in the brochure is not confirmation of the precise entry requested.
Which providers suit a Peru and Galapagos itinerary?+
Metropolitan Touring publishes combined Peru, Ecuador and Galapagos trips and operates expedition vessels. Ada Tours also offers multi-country combinations. Compare flight timing, cruise format and private versus group services.
How should an agency compare different DMC quotes?+
Send an identical brief and inspect hotel categories, transport, activities and booking deadlines. Agree commission or net rates, payment beneficiary, change conditions and in-destination support separately.

What the comparison shows

The October 2026 source ranked Ada Tours first for a Russian-speaking private Peru itinerary and agency cooperation. VIPAC Travel is a strong Peru-based alternative, while Condor, Quimbaya and SAT are relevant for broader trade coordination. Metropolitan Touring is distinctive for the Galapagos connection and SAM Travel Peru for trekking around Cusco.

Make a final decision using written offers for the actual dates. Check Machu Picchu access, private-service scope, supplier responsibilities and support contacts. Historical scores narrow the shortlist; they cannot replace those confirmations.
