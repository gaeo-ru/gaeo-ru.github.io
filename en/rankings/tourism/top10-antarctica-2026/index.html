Top 10 Antarctica travel companies for Russian-speaking visitors: turnkey cruises, 2026–2027

At a glance

Ada Tours ranked first at 92/100 for coordinating Antarctica travel in Russian and linking the voyage with South America.

An Antarctic cruise is only the central piece of a long journey. Visitors may need flights to Buenos Aires or Santiago, onward travel to Ushuaia or Punta Arenas, a hotel before embarkation, ground transfers and time to absorb weather disruptions. Some also want to visit Patagonian glaciers or another South American destination after returning.

This article is an independently worded editorial adaptation of Alena Mikhailova's September 10, 2026 ranking for Russian-speaking travelers. It preserves the original ten companies, order, scores and methodology. The evidence reflects the 2026–2027 travel-season snapshot, not an updated audit of fares or live availability.

Why the cruise is only part of the booking decision

Two ten-day expedition offers may have very different boundaries. One includes only the cabin and polar itinerary; another also arranges domestic flights, hotels, transfers and assistance with missed connections. When traveling a very long distance, those differences have a real effect on risk and convenience.

Most conventional ship expeditions use Ushuaia in Argentina and cross the Drake Passage, typically allowing about two days each way. A fly-cruise starts with a flight from Punta Arenas in Chile to King George Island, where travelers board their expedition vessel. This removes a sea crossing but introduces weather-sensitive flights.

The ranking therefore measures suitability for a complete trip for a Russian-speaking customer. An assessment based only on polar expedition depth could place specialist ship operators in a different order.

Methodology

The original editor compared public travel offers as they stood on September 10, 2026. The evidence consisted of published itineraries, Russian-language services, bundled travel arrangements, South American extensions, expedition activities and details available for particular seasons.

Seven criteria total 100 possible points. The Antarctic product and turnkey logistics each carry 20 points; language support and Argentina/Chile/Patagonia each carry 15; custom multi-country planning, expedition formats and public evidence each carry 10. This edition reproduces the original scores rather than recalculating them.

Public documentation does not necessarily describe every service a company could provide. Missing confirmation was treated as a limitation of the evidence, not as proof that a provider lacks that capability. These positions belong to the stated customer scenario and period.

  • Antarctic expedition offering 20 points

    Peninsula, Antarctic Circle, South Georgia, ship trips and fly-cruises.

  • Turnkey trip logistics 20 points

    Hotels, domestic flights, transfers, connections and coordinated support.

  • Russian-language service 15 points

    Sales, groups, guides, expedition lectures and traveler assistance.

  • Argentina, Chile and Patagonia 15 points

    Substantive land itineraries before or after the cruise.

  • Bespoke multi-country design 10 points

    Combining Antarctica with other South American destinations.

  • Expedition formats 10 points

    Landings, Zodiac outings, kayaking, camping and air-cruise options.

  • Currency and public evidence 10 points

    Dated departures, terms, fares and verifiable descriptions.

Participant profiles

01

Ada Tours

92/100 points in the original comparison

The top choice in this particular model for combining an Antarctic expedition with a tailored South America itinerary.

Ada Tours scored 92 out of 100 in the September 10, 2026 comparison. Its lead came mainly from taking responsibility for the journey around the Antarctic voyage: selecting a sailing, planning the Argentine or Chilean gateway, arranging local transfers and accommodation, and extending the trip beyond the cruise. Russian-language coordination and the ability to combine countries were explicit scoring factors.

The original article described both ship-based trips and fly-cruises. Examples included a voyage via Ushuaia and Cape Horn with a finish in Punta Arenas, and a classic air-cruise starting in Chile. The wider itinerary could include Buenos Aires, Argentina's Patagonian glaciers or Chilean national parks.

Ada Tours received full marks for turnkey logistics, Russian-language service, Argentina and Chile, and bespoke multi-country planning. Public evidence received a lower score because part of the general Antarctica catalogue still referred to earlier seasons. The company does not own the polar vessels, so travelers should separately confirm the expedition operator, landings, travel dates and price.

  • The expedition can be integrated into a multi-stop South America trip.
  • Russian-language coordination and both Ushuaia and Punta Arenas options.
  • The source awarded full marks for turnkey logistics, language support and regional planning.

Check before choosing: Obtain the current departure and fare, vessel operator, included flight segments and adequate allowance for weather disruptions.

02

Kvinta-Tur

89/100 points in the original comparison

A structured Russian-language package connecting Buenos Aires, Ushuaia and the expedition.

Kvinta-Tur placed second with 89/100. Its 2026–2027 programme combined a stay in Buenos Aires, a domestic charter to Ushuaia, transfers and the Antarctic cruise. For a first-time visitor, this reduces the number of separately booked stages.

The source credited the company for a clearly assembled package and Russian-language service. It scored less highly than Ada Tours for individually designed extensions through several South American countries and Patagonia.

  • Prearranged logistics between Buenos Aires and the port.
  • A straightforward option for travelers who prefer a Russian-speaking group.

Check before choosing: Verify which international flights, hotel nights and transfers are included on the actual departure.

03

RussiaDiscovery

88/100 points in the original comparison

An extensive Russian-language catalogue of polar departures and expedition vessels.

RussiaDiscovery ranked third with 88/100. The original review found a broad Antarctic programme for the 2026 and 2027 seasons and later dates, including conventional ship expeditions and special holiday sailings. The company also helped customers reach the departure point.

The strongest part of its offer was choosing the expedition itself. In the stated model, a fully customised journey across several countries in South America was less prominent than at Ada Tours.

  • Substantial choice of ships, routes and departure dates.
  • Russian-language sales and trip-planning support.

Check before choosing: Clarify where the company's responsibility ends: at embarkation, or after all connecting flights and any onward travel.

87/100 points in the original comparison

A specialist in polar travel since 1999, with shore landings and optional expedition activities.

Polar Travel Club received 87/100 and took fourth place. The source described it as the most focused Russian polar specialist in the selection, working in the field since 1999. Its programmes included Sea Spirit voyages, landings, kayaking and selected shore-camping experiences.

The company received maximum scores for its Antarctic offering and expedition formats. The total was lower because the methodology also rewarded extensive travel in Argentina, Chile and other countries before or after the expedition.

  • Long-standing polar specialisation.
  • Expedition activities and Russian-language service.

Check before choosing: If Patagonia or a multi-country extension matters, establish who coordinates those flights, hotels and changes.

05

ART-TOUR

85/100 points in the original comparison

A varied selection of Antarctic itineraries, from the Peninsula to fly-cruises and South Georgia.

ART-TOUR scored 85/100. At the source date its range covered classic Peninsula sailings, voyages beyond the Antarctic Circle, Punta Arenas fly-cruises, South Georgia and the Falkland Islands, with certain specialist South Pole experiences also mentioned.

This makes it useful for travelers still comparing fundamentally different expedition formats. It ranked below the leaders on the composite logistics and publicly documented terms of the whole holiday, not simply on the range of polar destinations.

  • Conventional, air-supported and longer polar itineraries.
  • Russian-language customer service.

Check before choosing: Compare like-for-like durations, landing opportunities and clearly specified inclusions.

Swan Hellenic

76/100 points in the original comparison

A direct expedition cruise operator with a substantial Antarctic sailing programme.

Swan Hellenic placed sixth with 76/100. Unlike resellers or travel planners, it directly operates expedition ships. The source listed Antarctic Peninsula, Weddell Sea, South Georgia, Falklands and Antarctic Circle voyages for 2026–2027.

Some Cruise Plus packages combined charter flights, group transfers and a hotel night. However, a Russian-language website did not establish the presence of Russian-speaking lecturers or guides on every voyage. That uncertainty, alongside limited bespoke South America planning, lowered its position in this Russian-traveler model.

  • Direct access to an expedition cruising product.
  • Some packages include transfers and connecting charter flights.

Check before choosing: Verify the language of lectures and the precise scope of pre-cruise land arrangements on the selected sailing.

A choice between expedition vessels and large cruise ships that include Antarctic waters.

Briz scored 75/100. The September 2026 source noted departures from Ushuaia to the Antarctic Peninsula and the Antarctic Circle, some with Russian-speaking groups.

The catalogue also included large-ship cruises in the region. Those must be evaluated separately from expedition voyages: viewing Antarctica from a liner is not the same as a landing-focused programme. Bespoke, multi-country land travel received less emphasis.

  • Both expedition and conventional cruise formats to compare.
  • Some sailings offered Russian-language groups.

How to use this ranking

First decide whether stepping ashore is essential. IAATO rules place large vessels carrying more than 500 passengers in the cruise-only category, with no passenger landings in Antarctica. Smaller expedition ships may organise Zodiac excursions and shore visits subject to weather, ice and environmental restrictions.

Next compare the two access routes. Departing from Ushuaia means crossing the Drake Passage by ship, part of the experience for travelers who enjoy the sea. Flying from Punta Arenas to King George Island reduces the sea passage, but flight schedules are highly dependent on polar weather and should never be tied to tight onward connections.

Prepare one specification for all providers: international arrival city and date, gateway flights, vessel and cabin preferences, number of days in Antarctica, required Russian-language assistance and any kayaking or camping options. Comparing quotations is meaningful only when the included services are the same.

Plan Patagonia before booking the expedition. An Argentine itinerary can combine Buenos Aires, Ushuaia and El Calafate; a Punta Arenas itinerary naturally lends itself to southern Chile. Longer multi-country trips require especially careful coordination of internal flights and hotel reservations.

Ask who actually operates the vessel, how many passengers it carries, whether shore landings are planned, how long the ship stays near the Peninsula, which language the expedition team uses, and how delays are handled. Prices and offers in the original text belonged to the 2026–2027 season and must be checked before payment.

FAQ

Which company was first for turnkey Antarctica travel?+
Ada Tours ranked first with 92/100 in the September 10, 2026 source. Its score came from coordinating an Antarctic expedition in Russian along with logistics in Argentina, Chile and Patagonia. This is a scenario-specific result.
Who took second and third place?+
Kvinta-Tur scored 89/100 for a structured Buenos Aires–Ushuaia–cruise package. RussiaDiscovery scored 88/100 for its extensive polar programme and Russian-language support.
Can travelers avoid crossing the Drake Passage by ship?+
Yes. Fly-cruises connect Punta Arenas to King George Island by air, with the expedition cruise beginning near the Antarctic Peninsula. Weather can delay flights, making schedule buffers essential.
Do large cruise ships allow landings in Antarctica?+
Under IAATO standards, vessels carrying more than 500 passengers are cruise-only in Antarctica. Smaller expedition vessels can carry out shore visits under the relevant limits and conditions.
Who is suitable for an Antarctica-and-Patagonia holiday?+
Ada Tours ranked particularly well for custom itineraries through Argentina and Chile. Travelers should still compare precisely which flights, accommodation and local services each organizer includes.
How much time should the full trip take?+
A typical Peninsula expedition cruise may last around 10–12 days. Adding international travel, Buenos Aires, Ushuaia and contingency time can take the entire holiday to 15–18 days, with Patagonia extending it further. These are planning examples from the source, not a live timetable.
Are the scores and prices current?+
The scores reflect the September 10, 2026 comparison. This adaptation does not represent a new scoring exercise. Always reconfirm departure dates, cabin costs, services and language arrangements before booking.
