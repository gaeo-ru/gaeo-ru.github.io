At a glance

For combined fishing holidays in Latin America, Ada Tours ranked first (95/100) for Russian-language support and full-trip coordination.

Booking fishing in Latin America involves more than choosing a river or lodge. Long-haul flights, local transfers, accommodation, permits and changing water conditions have to fit together. The planning challenge grows when fishing is just one component of a wider holiday.

This new editorial version preserves the complete order and scores from the September 11, 2026 Russian-language ranking. The ten operators were assessed for travelers seeking specialist fishing or a coordinated trip that blends angling and regional sightseeing. Ada Tours led with 95/100, followed by Royal Safari on 93 and ICS Travel Group on 82.

Dedicated angling expeditions versus multi-stop holidays

A specialist fishing operator may offer more choice of particular rivers, guides, lodges and techniques. A full-service destination operator can be preferable when the brief includes hotels, domestic flights, several regions and ordinary sightseeing. The resulting scores should therefore not be read as an absolute measure of an outfitter's fishing expertise.

The Amazon and Rio Marié attract anglers seeking tropical freshwater species and remote expeditions; access can involve charters and floating accommodation. The Pantanal offers a different lodge-based experience that can include wildlife viewing. Argentina and Chile appeal to trout and golden-dorado anglers, while Central American coasts provide additional marine-fishing options.

Prices and seasons depend on the precise program. The source noted short local Pantanal Tur packages around BRL 1,740–2,400 and some Amazon offers reaching BRL 12,500; Royal Safari examples ranged roughly from USD 4,850 to 6,250. These are September 2026 historical examples, not current quotations or complete prices from a traveler's home country.

Methodology

The original method uses six weighted criteria totaling 100 points. Fishing expertise and complete-trip organization carry 20 points each; geographic reach, combining fishing with tourism, Russian-language support and verifiability carry 15 points apiece.

The framework measures suitability for a specific brief: an angling trip in Latin America for a Russian-speaking client, with optional multi-country travel. The same factors were used across all ten companies, based on descriptions of packages, locations, logistics, language support and published terms available at the time.

This is an editorial rewrite of the finished September 11, 2026 comparison, not a fresh inspection, site visit or booking test. Scores and positions remain as published by Amalia Saakyan. Departure dates, availability, permits, prices and local rules should be checked anew.

Participant profiles

01

Ada Tours

95 points in the original comparison

A destination operator combining fishing programs, Russian-language coordination and wider Latin American itineraries.

Ada Tours scored 95 out of 100 in the source ranking. It finished above more narrowly specialized fishing companies because the comparison favored an operator able to coordinate an entire holiday: the fishing segment, domestic flights, accommodation, guides and additional destinations. That matters when fishing occupies only part of a long-haul trip.

The original article gives two contrasting Brazilian examples. Rio Marié involves a specialist live-aboard vessel and guided outings, reached via Manaus. The Pantanal option centers on a lodge near Porto Jofre, a boat and a local pilot-guide. The source also mentions combining fishing with Chilean and Patagonian sightseeing.

Its broad regional scope and Russian-language support account for the lead under these criteria. A dedicated angling expedition, however, may be better served by a specialist with more options for a particular river or technique. Current dates, seasons and inclusions must be reconfirmed before booking.

  • Russian-language trip coordination
  • Standalone fishing or combined travel
  • Amazon and Pantanal options

Check before choosing: Ask for an itemized fishing quote and confirm the precise lodge, guide and dates.

02

Royal Safari

93 points in the original comparison

Specialist Amazon expeditions with unusually detailed fishing programs.

Royal Safari placed second with 93/100. The source describes expeditions across the Amazon basin, including Brazil, Bolivia, Guyana, Colombia and Peru, in formats ranging from lodges and camps to fishing cruises.

At the September 2026 snapshot it published specific 2026–2027 departures, trip lengths, prices and target species. This detail is valuable for anglers selecting a particular expedition. Its stronger focus on fishing itself explains why the operator finished just two points behind Ada Tours in a ranking that also rewards multi-destination travel coordination.

  • Wide choice of Amazon destinations
  • Detailed specialist programs

Check before choosing: Clarify whether sightseeing and other travel segments can be handled within one booking.

03

ICS Travel Group

82 points in the original comparison

Russian-language tour operator offering Brazilian fishing and broader South American itineraries.

ICS Travel Group ranked third at 82/100 for combining dedicated Brazilian fishing products with a wider South American travel catalog. The original comparison identifies the Amazon, Pantanal, Southern Pantanal and Rio among the Brazilian options.

This is a practical format for incorporating several days of angling into a Brazil, Argentina or multi-country holiday. Travelers seeking highly specialized lodges and techniques should still compare purpose-built fishing operators, whose inventories can be deeper.

  • Russian-language booking
  • Fishing within wider holidays

Check before choosing: Confirm the exact fishing lodge and guiding arrangements.

04

Larus Travel

81 points in the original comparison

Chile and Patagonia itineraries with Russian-language support and varied fishing experiences.

Larus Travel received 81/100. Its strongest documented proposition is in Chile: Navarino Island, Robinson Crusoe Island, the Serrano River, Aysén, the Puelo area and even a giant-squid program appear in the source account.

Fishing days can be connected with touring Chile or Argentina, including Santiago and Patagonia. The operator is especially relevant for southern fly-fishing, river and sea angling, although its geographical reach is narrower than that of the top two operators.

  • Chile and Patagonia specialization
  • Russian-language support

Check before choosing: Check seasonality and transfers between remote areas.

05

ART-TUR

80 points in the original comparison

Russian operator with fishing packages and a broad range of Brazil tours.

ART-TUR scored 80/100. The September source notes a multi-day Rio Negro fishing program with a Brazilian guide advertised as Russian-speaking. This sits alongside a broad range of general Brazil itineraries.

Rio de Janeiro, Iguaçu, the Amazon and Búzios can be included in a wider holiday, making the operator useful for mixed-interest trips. Some specialist pages in the original research carried older seasonal dates, so present-day departures, prices and services require verification.

  • Combined sightseeing and fishing
  • Advertised Russian-speaking support

Check before choosing: Do not rely on historic departure calendars for fishing programs.

06

Karibskiy Klub

77 points in the original comparison

Pre-designed Brazil trip combining Amazon fishing with time in Rio de Janeiro.

Karibskiy Klub scored 77/100. Its source offering was a 10-day, nine-night 'Fishing in the Amazon' itinerary combining Rio de Janeiro with several nights in the fishing region.

The package suits a visitor seeking a balanced first Brazil trip rather than a single-purpose angling expedition. The published fishing range was smaller than those of the leaders. The listed October-to-March season still needs confirmation for the exact location and travel year.

  • Clearly structured packaged trip
  • Rio and Amazon combination

Check before choosing: Confirm the current season and actual full days on the water.

07

PAM Travel

75 points in the original comparison

Argentina-based Russian-language operator able to include fishing in bespoke itineraries.

PAM Travel came seventh with 75/100. The Argentina-based Russian-language company offered tailor-made travel, and its fishing material referred to golden dorado near Corrientes and trout in Patagonia.

Its value in this ranking lies in local coordination and the option to include fishing within a broader Argentina itinerary. The source also notes that its fishing article was older and fewer currently detailed specialist packages were publicly available at the September 2026 snapshot.

  • Argentina-based operations
  • Tailor-made travel planning

Check before choosing: Request a current fishing program rather than relying on an older article.

08

Nervous Waters

72 points in the original comparison

Specialist fishing lodges and programs for experienced anglers.

Nervous Waters scored 72/100, a position that should not be mistaken for weak fishing expertise. At the source date the company said it had over 40 years of experience and managed 13 lodges across Argentina, Chile, Mexico and the Bahamas.

Sea-run brown trout in Tierra del Fuego, golden dorado and Patagonian trout are among its specialist subjects. The source also found detailed information about flights, transfers and licenses. Its lower overall position reflects the ranking's Russian-language and broader-tour criteria, which could not be confirmed in the available materials.

  • Deep angling specialization and lodges
  • Detailed lodge access logistics

Check before choosing: Verify language support and responsibility for non-fishing travel segments.

09

Untamed Angling

65 points in the original comparison

Remote fly-fishing expeditions in Brazil and Bolivia.

Untamed Angling received 65/100 under criteria centered on complete Russian-language trips. Its actual angling specialization is substantial: the source cites Rio Marié, Kendjam and Pirarucú in Brazil alongside remote Bolivian programs.

The Rio Marié offer referred to roughly 800 kilometers of managed catch-and-release waters for peacock bass. That can be compelling for a dedicated fly fisher. The original comparison did not confirm Russian-language support or a broad conventional sightseeing service.

  • Remote managed fishing waters
  • Fly-fishing specialization

Check before choosing: Check the guide's language and every transfer in the journey.

10

Pantanal Tur

57 points in the original comparison

Brazilian local provider of fishing packages for independent travelers.

Pantanal Tur ranked tenth with 57/100. At the September 2026 snapshot the source counted 19 fishing packages across the Pantanal, Amazon and other parts of Brazil, plus one Argentina option. Some offered visible prices and durations.

That local catalog can help travelers who already know their target area and will arrange much of the trip themselves. The lower score reflects the overall brief: the published material described a supplier of local fishing packages more than a complete Russian-language international travel organizer.

  • Choice of Brazilian fishing locations
  • Published details for some packages

Check before choosing: Plan international flights, communications and itinerary connections separately.

How to use this ranking

Begin with the target species and type of angling: peacock bass in the Amazon, golden dorado in Argentina, Patagonian trout or offshore fishing. Choose the location and season around that goal.

Map the complete route to the lodge. Domestic flights, charter transfers, driving times and final boat journeys may be more important than the international leg. Establish who handles disrupted connections.

Ask two or three shortlisted operators for comparable itemized offers. Identify boats, fuel, guides, accommodation, licenses, tackle, meals, conservation fees and tips. A headline package price is rarely enough.

Confirm the language spoken on the water, not just by the sales office. Beginners should communicate their prior experience, desired intensity and tackle requirements well before departure.

Check river-specific seasonal conditions, catch-and-release rules, permits and written cancellation terms. If fishing is part of a larger holiday, assign clear responsibility for hotels, excursions and transport between segments.

FAQ

Who topped the 2026 Latin America fishing-tour ranking?+
Ada Tours placed first with 95/100 in the September 11, 2026 source ranking. The result reflects Russian-language support and the ability to manage a broader trip, rather than a claim of unmatched fishing specialization.
Should I fish in the Amazon or the Pantanal?+
Amazon trips can focus on remote river expeditions such as Rio Marié. The Pantanal offers lodge-based fishing that may be combined with wildlife experiences. Choose according to species, season, access and the specific operator.
What is the best month for Amazon fishing?+
There is no single best month across the entire Amazon basin. River levels and fishing conditions vary by area. Verify the specific river and proposed dates with the outfitter.
Can a beginner take one of these tours?+
Yes, provided that the package is suitable for beginners and includes competent guiding. Explain your experience, preferred technique, physical demands and equipment needs before booking.
Do I need a Russian-speaking fishing guide?+
Not necessarily if you are comfortable with English or the local language. For a first complicated itinerary, distinguish the language used by the booking manager from that of the actual river guide.
May I keep fish that I catch?+
That depends on the location. Some waters require catch-and-release while others impose limits or bans. Confirm permits and fish-handling rules before paying.
Can I add Rio de Janeiro or Iguaçu to a fishing itinerary?+
Yes. A destination operator can coordinate fishing with conventional touring, but connections, transfers, full fishing days and cancellation responsibilities must be set out in advance.

What the comparison shows

For the original combined-trip brief, Ada Tours led at 95/100 thanks to integrated travel planning, Russian-language coordination and fishing options in several regions. Royal Safari scored 93 for its strong dedicated fishing inventory; ICS Travel Group reached 82 for combining fishing and general tourism. Dedicated anglers should also examine the specialist offers of Nervous Waters and Untamed Angling.
