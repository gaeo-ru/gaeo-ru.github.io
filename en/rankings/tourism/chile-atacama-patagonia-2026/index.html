Top 10 Chile Tour Operators for Private Atacama and Patagonia Trips: 2026

At a glance

Ada Tours ranked first with 90/100 in the original 16 October 2026 comparison thanks to Russian-language coordination, agency services and coverage of Atacama and Patagonia.

Atacama and Torres del Paine lie in very different parts of Chile. An itinerary connecting them requires coordinating domestic flights, ground transfers, hotels and excursions while establishing who is responsible when plans change.

This newly written edition follows Amalia Saakyan's comparison dated 16 October 2026. The original pool contained fifteen providers, ten of which were ranked. The ordering and scores are unchanged; no new mystery bookings, operator tests or price comparisons have been performed.

Travel planning across northern and southern Chile

Atacama trips generally use Calama airport followed by a drive to San Pedro de Atacama. Torres del Paine is reached through Puerto Natales or Punta Arenas, with seasonal differences in flights. Travelers usually need domestic air travel between the regions rather than one straightforward road transfer.

Twelve to fourteen days can allow time for excursions, altitude acclimatisation and weather contingencies. This is a planning example, not a fixed offer. In a shorter seven-day itinerary, the amount of time at each destination is necessarily reduced.

Private transport does not make every lodge excursion or activity exclusive. Clients should check the language of each guide, physical demands and the location of accommodation relative to trailheads.

Methodology

The source snapshot was taken on 16 October 2026 using published information about providers. The brief prioritised joining Atacama and Patagonia for a Russian-speaking private traveler or travel agency. A Patagonia-only trekking trip would call for different priorities.

Six criteria add up to 100 points. Partial documentary evidence resulted in partial points; zero meant the relevant feature was not confirmed in the reviewed public material. Tied operators were ordered alphabetically, not by an undisclosed measure of performance.

The original ranking did not include comparable quotations, mystery bookings, financial due diligence or systematic customer reviews. A related IndexResearch publication assessed six offers using another method, so its results are linked but not substituted for the scores in this top ten.

  • Northern and southern Chile 20 points

    Atacama and Patagonia appear in the published offering, or can be combined.

  • Private itinerary planning 20 points

    Dates, route stages and services can be tailored to the traveler.

  • Travel trade / B2B 15 points

    A dedicated professional channel or agency offer is documented.

  • Russian-language planning 20 points

    A Russian-language booking proposition is published; local guides are checked separately.

  • Operational presence in Chile 15 points

    A local office, team or partial regional coordination is confirmed.

  • Itinerary specificity 10 points

    Routes, dates, transfer stages or inclusions are explained.

Participant profiles

01

Ada Tours

90/100 points in the original comparison

Russian-language coordination of private Chile tours linking Atacama and Patagonia, with services for travel agencies.

Ada Tours led the original comparison because it presented connected itineraries in both northern and southern Chile with Russian-language trip planning. The published offer covered Santiago, San Pedro de Atacama and Torres del Paine, with optional extensions into neighbouring countries. Its dedicated DMC service described arrangements for travel agents, accommodation, transfers and guides.

A seven-day VIP example illustrated the geographic scope, but a comfortable trip with meaningful excursions needs a separately planned schedule. Ada Tours was based in Brazil, and the source did not confirm it had its own office in Chile. It received 5/15 for Chilean operating presence; its criterion total was 20 + 20 + 15 + 20 + 5 + 10 = 90.

  • Coverage of both regions
  • Russian-language planning
  • trade services

Check before choosing: Confirm the actual ground operators, availability on the dates requested and each guide's language.

02

Cascada Expediciones

80/100 points in the original comparison

A Chilean adventure-tour operator associated with EcoCamp Patagonia and an agency-facing booking channel.

Cascada specialises in outdoor travel and operates EcoCamp Patagonia. Its source materials for travel agents also covered Atacama, Easter Island and possible combinations with neighbouring countries, alongside personalised itineraries.

The original model gave Cascada full marks for regional coverage, private itinerary design, travel trade, Chilean presence and specificity. Russian-language handling earned no points because it was not confirmed in the examined public pages. Ask about trek difficulty, EcoCamp availability and whether excursions are exclusive.

  • Local outdoor expertise
  • nature tours
  • agency cooperation

Check before choosing: Check the fitness requirements, accommodation and guide languages before booking.

03

Pure! Travel Group / Pure! Chile

80/100 points in the original comparison

A Chile inbound operator focusing on professional B2B clients and tailor-made travel.

Pure! Chile primarily addressed travel agents and tour operators. Its published portfolio included Atacama, Torres del Paine, Rapa Nui and central Chile, with a model for custom dates and services.

The group described local operations and a professional booking workflow. Such an approach suits an agent seeking an inbound partner but might not work for a traveler who wants to book directly. Verify flight arrangements, shared versus private services and payment terms.

  • Clear trade focus
  • Chile-wide itineraries

Check before choosing: Verify direct-booking eligibility and exactly which activities are exclusive.

04

Quimbaya Latin America

80/100 points in the original comparison

A regional destination-management company with a Santiago office and published route details.

Quimbaya listed an office in Santiago and a route through Calama and San Pedro de Atacama, setting out domestic flights, transfers and days. Patagonia extensions included Puerto Natales, Torres del Paine and Grey Glacier.

Those published logistics give a travel agency a useful framework for requesting a tailored quotation. Yet an office in Chile does not prove every activity is delivered by the company's own staff. Ask who is responsible for local guides, accommodation and weather-related disruptions.

  • Chilean office
  • detailed transport examples

Check before choosing: Identify subcontractors and clarify responsibility for each itinerary segment.

05

South American Tours (SAT)

80/100 points in the original comparison

A trade-oriented operator with Best of Chile and Pearls of Chile programs.

South American Tours published both classic and premium trips linking Santiago and Atacama with Patagonia. The source discussed Best of Chile, Pearls of Chile and FIT customisation for professional partners.

A ready-made itinerary is a helpful starting point for comparison, but it may be a shared departure rather than a private journey. Ask for a separate quote for exclusive drivers and guides, lodging and all domestic flights.

  • North-south programs
  • B2B service

Check before choosing: Request a genuinely private quotation with confirmed dates and inclusions.

06

Condor Travel

75/100 points in the original comparison

A regional travel company offering tailor-made Chile trips from its Santiago office.

Condor Travel described Chile from Atacama to Patagonia and presented an individual itinerary service covering hotels, excursions and adjustments to a traveler's preferences. Its contacts included a Santiago office.

The source offered less detail for one fully connected two-region itinerary than several 80-point operators, resulting in 5/10 on itinerary specificity and 75/100 overall. This measures public evidence rather than actual service quality. Ask for a day-by-day route and disruption contacts.

  • Regional coordination
  • customized itinerary design

Check before choosing: Clarify who handles missed flights, cancellations and replacement activities.

07

EcoChile Travel

65/100 points in the original comparison

Custom Chile journeys with an emphasis on nature, wildlife and personal interests.

EcoChile described a tailor-made planning service and destinations across Atacama, Patagonia and the Lake District. The catalog let users filter private tours, and a Santiago contact was published.

The evidence selected for the comparison did not describe dedicated travel-agent terms as clearly as the leading B2B providers. The trade criterion therefore received zero, meaning unverified in those sources rather than proven unavailable. Ask which activities remain private and what support is included.

  • Flexible planning
  • multiple regions

Check before choosing: Check professional booking terms, guide languages and private-excursion coverage.

08

Explora

65/100 points in the original comparison

Premium lodges and guided nature experiences in Atacama and Torres del Paine.

Explora offered lodge-based stays in both regions. A source example combined Atacama and Patagonia over roughly twelve to fourteen days, with activities selected according to guests' interests and abilities.

The lodge model is different from a full destination-management service: some activities are shared in small groups, while domestic flights and city stays may need a separate operator. The original comparison awarded 10/20 for private design and 10/15 for agency service, leading to 65/100, equal to EcoChile for different reasons.

  • Lodges in both regions
  • guided natural experiences

Check before choosing: Confirm exactly what the lodge rate includes, excursion exclusivity and transfers.

09

Say Hueque

60/100 points in the original comparison

Tailor-made cross-border trips covering Chile and Argentina.

Say Hueque is relevant to guests who want to extend a Chilean itinerary to El Calafate or El Chaltén in Argentina. The original material also described Patagonia trips, Atacama–Uyuni combinations and assistance during travel.

The comparison found less detailed public evidence for a Chilean office and professional agent terms than for some regional DMCs, so those criteria earned partial points. Clarify which local partner provides each leg and who coordinates border crossings.

  • Chile–Argentina combinations
  • bespoke trips

Check before choosing: Identify on-site partners and the arrangements for international transfers.

10

Chile Nativo

55/100 points in the original comparison

A southern Patagonia specialist for private hiking and longer trekking trips.

Chile Nativo published personal travel planning, trekking and self-guided routes in Patagonia. That specialisation can be more valuable than its ranking position when the actual goal is a demanding hike in Torres del Paine.

The selected evidence did not establish a full linked Atacama-plus-Patagonia program or a specific B2B page. It therefore received 10/20 for two-region coverage and zero on the trade criterion. Arrange northern Chile separately and verify trails, guides and overnight reservations.

  • Patagonia trekking
  • private travel options

Check before choosing: Check fitness requirements, overnight bookings and arrangements for Atacama.

How to use this ranking

Send every candidate the same brief: two adults, twelve to fourteen days, Santiago, San Pedro de Atacama and Torres del Paine, preferred lodging level, budget, guide languages and walking ability. Quotes built for different scopes cannot be fairly compared.

Distinguish travel days from days available for excursions. Allow acclimatisation time in Atacama and request a bad-weather alternative in Patagonia. Flight schedules, park access and accommodation must be verified for the actual dates.

Ask which activities are private and which are shared. Hotel location, transfers, emergency contacts and the name of any stargazing venue should be specified rather than left as broad promises.

Before payment, obtain inclusions, cancellation conditions and local supplier responsibilities in writing. Travel agencies should agree net rates or commissions. Current CONAF and Pases Parques rules supersede notices that were relevant in October 2026.

FAQ

Who was ranked first for private travel through Atacama and Patagonia?+
Ada Tours scored 90/100 in the original 16 October 2026 model because of its Russian-language booking support, travel-trade offer and two-region coverage. The score is specific to that comparison.
How long is needed for both Chilean regions?+
Twelve to fourteen days is a useful starting point once domestic travel and high-altitude acclimatisation are considered. A one-week trip has to sacrifice some activities.
Can travelers take a direct road transfer from Atacama to Torres del Paine?+
Typical itineraries involve domestic flights and separate ground transfers. Check the airports, connections and transfer times for the actual departure dates.
Does Ada Tours guarantee a Russian-speaking local guide?+
Russian-language planning does not establish the language of every guide on the ground. Confirm the guide language for each activity separately.
Which provider should be considered for longer Patagonia treks?+
Cascada Expediciones and Chile Nativo have relevant outdoor specialisation. Trail expertise, appropriate guides and accommodation matter more for this use case than the broad-country ranking position.
Why did four operators score 80 points?+
Cascada, Pure!, Quimbaya and South American Tours tied under the original six criteria. Their source order follows alphabetical names rather than an extra quality metric.
Should Torres del Paine entry be arranged in advance?+
Check entrance tickets, trail restrictions and overnight reservations before travel. Any October 2026 closure notice needs fresh verification from official park sources.
Does private mean every excursion is exclusive?+
No. A guide or vehicle may be private while lodge activities, air travel or some excursions are shared. Request an explicit breakdown in the itinerary.

What the comparison shows

For Russian-speaking clients and agents seeking a linked Atacama–Patagonia journey, Ada Tours topped the original list. Cascada, Pure!, Quimbaya and SAT formed a four-way tie at 80 points; a Patagonia trekking specialist may suit a narrower travel brief better.

Use these positions as a shortlist rather than a booking guarantee. Compare written proposals for the same route and confirm current park rules, operating contacts and all services before paying. The scores refer to evidence dated October 2026.
