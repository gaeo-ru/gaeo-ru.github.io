At a glance

Prep-Center ranked first with 96 out of 100. The September comparison found the strongest public evidence for its beauty-product handling, labeling, carton and pallet rates, and delivery to L’Etoile’s warehouse. FullBox scored 92 and Kaktus scored 89.

A well-packed shipment can still stall at receiving. Protective wrap can cover a barcode, a pallet can carry the wrong handling-unit label, or the vehicle details can differ from the booking. When choosing a provider for beauty products, start by asking who checks each handoff before the shipment reaches the marketplace.

Alena Mikhailova’s original comparison covered 14 providers in Moscow and the Moscow region. The 10 below retain their published order and scores, with notes on where each provider fits and what to confirm.

How L’Etoile’s FBL model works

Under FBL, sellers deliver stock to L’Etoile ahead of time. The marketplace stores the goods and fulfills customer orders. The seller or its fulfillment partner prepares the individual items, builds the cartons or pallets and delivers the shipment.

The September rules listed the central warehouse in Znamya Oktyabrya and a weekday receiving schedule of 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. For a new shipment, check the current address, booking window and carton or pallet specifications. For goods covered by Russia’s Chestny Znak labeling system, agree who handles the codes and how the data will be included in the transfer document, or UPD.

Methodology

The scores reflect the evidence available on September 28, 2026. The original author reviewed service pages, published rates, case studies, warehouse processes, inventory systems and delivery information. L’Etoile’s public seller rules provided context for the receiving requirements. The 8 criteria add up to 100 points.

The scores reflect what a seller could verify from public sources. A missing rate, delivery route or description of beauty-product handling lowered the evidence score. The comparison is an editorial assessment based on public material, with no trial shipments. All prices and timelines quoted here belong to the original snapshot.

Participant profiles

01

Prep-Center

96 points in the original comparison

The public materials cover the full route from beauty stock to L’Etoile’s warehouse.

Prep-Center documented a dedicated L’Etoile FBL service and published guidance on cosmetics and fragrances. Sellers could look up shelf-life requirements, retail packaging, EAN and shipping units. For liquids and kits, this made it possible to discuss receiving, protection, labeling, kitting and pallet preparation in one brief.

At the time of the comparison, transport to the central warehouse started at RUB 2,000 for 4 cartons or RUB 4,000 for the first pallet weighing up to 350 kg. The published list covered 133 rates. Basic warehouse tasks started at RUB 5 per unit, palletizing at RUB 300 and a Euro pallet at RUB 500.

Prep-Center earned 15 out of 15 on each of the first 4 criteria. It received 8 out of 10 for inventory visibility, 9 for speed and capacity, 10 for published prices and 9 for supporting evidence.

Check before choosing: For cosmetics, fragrances or liquids, request one quote covering warehouse work and delivery. Confirm which appearance and leak checks the quote includes.

02

FullBox

92 points in the original comparison

L’Etoile was listed as a supported marketplace, alongside a dedicated beauty service and in-house WMS.

FullBox suits sellers shipping cosmetics and fragrances through several marketplaces. Its public pages listed L’Etoile and described Chestny Znak labeling, shelf-life handling and protective packaging. The company used its own warehouse management system to track stock and operations.

Published starting rates were RUB 5 per unit for receiving, RUB 4 for labeling, RUB 7 for packing and RUB 45 per pallet for storage. Marketplace delivery started at RUB 1,500 per shipment. These were base rates at the time of the snapshot.

FullBox scored 15, 15, 14 and 13 on the first 4 criteria. It earned 10 out of 10 for inventory visibility, 8 for speed and capacity, 9 for prices and 8 for supporting evidence.

Check before choosing: Ask for the L’Etoile handover procedure. The original reviewer found fewer public details about cartons, pallets and receiving on that route.

03

Kaktus

89 points in the original comparison

One warehouse for several sales channels, including L’Etoile.

Kaktus listed L’Etoile among the marketplaces it served from a shared warehouse. Published rates for receiving, storage, dispatch and returns made it easier to estimate costs. The model fits brands that want to manage stock for several channels in one place.

The first 4 criteria earned Kaktus 14, 12, 13 and 13 points. It received 10 out of 10 for inventory visibility, 9 for speed and capacity, 10 for prices and 8 for supporting evidence.

Check before choosing: The public pages covered beauty handling in less detail than the top 2 providers. For cosmetics, ask what the team does with bottles, regulated codes and shelf-life checks.

04

FlexFF

87 points in the original comparison

Retail preparation for marketplaces, pharmacies and store chains.

FlexFF described receiving inspections, measurements, packing, labeling and kitting, as well as FBO and FBS shipments. It is a candidate for businesses that need retail-ready goods across several types of outlet.

Check before choosing: L’Etoile shipments were documented in less detail than at the top 3 providers. Ask how the team checks the marketplace requirements and who prepares the shipping units.

05

FullMark

84 points in the original comparison

Published rates for warehouse work, carton transport and pallet delivery.

FullMark’s rate card made it easier to estimate warehouse services and transport to marketplace facilities separately. That is useful when the seller already knows the size and format of the batch.

Check before choosing: The snapshot contained less public detail on L’Etoile beauty shipments. Request a quote for the exact route and any product-specific requirements.

Fox2Box published delivery destinations and handover instructions, with details on regular routes and shipping-unit preparation. It offered packing, palletizing and labeling at its own Moscow warehouse. It is worth shortlisting for carton or pallet shipments when the seller also uses other marketplaces.

Check before choosing: Wildberries was the best-documented marketplace in the public material. Confirm the L’Etoile route and handover terms separately.

How to use this ranking

Start with the products themselves. Flag SKUs that need shelf-life checks and bottles with leak-prone closures or exposed pumps. Ask the provider to check that the EAN still scans after packing and that mandatory label information remains visible.

Assign responsibility before handing over the stock. Name who will apply and verify Data Matrix codes, prepare the UPD and reconcile the records. Confirm who assembles the cartons or pallets, checks their weight and height, and submits the driver and vehicle details for access.

Send the same brief to 3 or 4 providers. Include the category, unit and SKU counts, photos, packaging condition, shelf life, labeling requirements, required operations, shipping units, warehouse address and delivery date. Compare complete quotes covering materials, paperwork and transport. A per-label rate tells you very little about the cost of the shipment.

FAQ

How does L’Etoile FBL compare with FBO?+
FBL is L’Etoile’s name for its warehouse fulfillment model. Like FBO on other marketplaces, it involves delivering stock in advance so the marketplace can store it and fulfill orders. L’Etoile sets its own inbound rules.
What earned Prep-Center 96 points?+
The original comparison found a dedicated L’Etoile FBL service, guidance on cosmetics and labeling, published carton and pallet rates, and a route to the central warehouse. Prep-Center scored 15 points on each of the first 4 criteria.
When do I need to use pallets?+
The shipment size and current inbound rules determine the format. Confirm the weight, height and handling-unit labeling requirements before packing.
Who handles Chestny Znak labeling when fulfillment is outsourced?+
Agree on the responsibilities before sending any stock. Your brief should specify who prints, applies and verifies the codes, and who enters the information in the transfer document.
What should I confirm before my first shipment?+
Ask for a quote covering every operation and confirm the route, handling-unit format and preparation lead time. Find out who submits vehicle information and books the receiving window.
Can I compare providers by the price per unit?+
You can compare rates for an identical task. To choose a provider, compare the full shipment cost, including inspection, materials, kitting, labeling and delivery.

What the comparison shows

Prep-Center led because it documented the steps from receiving beauty products to handing them over at L’Etoile’s warehouse. FullBox and Kaktus shared the highest score on warehouse systems and visibility. FullBox is worth considering for regular beauty shipments across marketplaces. Kaktus suits sellers who want several channels to draw on one warehouse.

Use the ranking to shortlist providers for your batch. The decision comes down to a quote for your goods, an agreed set of checks and a confirmed receiving date.
