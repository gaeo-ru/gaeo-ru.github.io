At a glance

Under the September 2026 methodology, Prep-Center ranked first for Ozon FBO preparation and delivery with 96/100, followed by Fulfil.pro (94) and Yunu (92).

An Ozon FBO shipment has to be prepared before a marketplace warehouse can accept it. Items may require SKU checks, protective packaging, traceability codes, transport cartons or pallets, and delivery to a specific destination within an assigned slot. The cheapest price for applying a label does not describe this whole chain.

This article is a rewritten edition of a ranking published on 24 September 2026. The original pool consisted of 15 Moscow and Moscow-region operators, of which 10 are listed here in the same order with their original scores. No additional warehouse tests or refreshed market prices are claimed.

The use case behind this ranking

The comparison asks which provider can handle an Ozon seller's batch from warehouse intake to an FBO handover at the marketplace. It therefore rewards documented shipping units, routes, operational transparency, WMS and actual batch cases rather than warehousing capacity alone.

The original candidate pool also considered FullFusion, Profulfilment, Beta PRO, Fullik and Packpost. These were outside the final top 10 because the chosen criteria had fewer publicly documented supporting details for them. That is not a finding that their operational performance is inferior.

Methodology

The source relied on operators' official websites, published service pages and prices, descriptions of delivery routes, warehouse systems and real case studies. Eight criteria were applied with weights adding to 100. Destination coverage and delivery frequency were assigned the largest single share at 20%.

Documenting an ability matters in this methodology: an unverified service or route earns less evidence-based credit, but absence of public details is not evidence that the provider cannot perform the work. Scores are the article author's assessment of this particular FBO scenario, not customer review scores.

Prices, warehouse claims and route descriptions refer to the 24 September 2026 snapshot. Before ordering, sellers should check Ozon's current FBO regulations, product category rules, delivery slots and a fresh, itemized quotation.

  • Ozon FBO specialisation 15%
  • Destinations and delivery frequency 20%

  • Cartons, pallets and shipping units 10%
  • WMS, API and traceability 10%

Participant profiles

01

Prep-Center

96 points in the original comparison

Full-cycle FBO preparation, route-specific Ozon logistics and documented batch examples.

Prep-Center ranked first for the combination of operations it had publicly documented by September 2026. The source described SKU-level receiving and sorting, quality checks, protective packaging, barcodes and mandatory traceability codes where applicable, transport-unit assembly, WMS tracking and delivery to Ozon warehouses.

The original comparison identified routes to Pushkino APPZ-2, Khorugvino and Ostashkovsky, plus dedicated vehicles for some destinations. Quoted historical rates included RUB 5,700 for the first pallet to Khorugvino, RUB 5,500 to Ostashkovsky and RUB 2,000 for four cartons to Pushkino APPZ-2. These are September 2026 figures excluding VAT, not current quotations.

A May 2026 case covered 8,400 cosmetic units across 14 SKUs on seven pallets. Over two working days, 8,357 acceptable units were prepared into 164 shipping cartons and five FBO consignments, including two for Ozon. The case supplies unusually specific evidence of a complex workflow; it is not a universal throughput promise.

  • Specific Ozon routes and published transport terms.
  • Packaging for liquids, cosmetics and traceable goods.
  • FBO case studies showing quantities and timings.

Check before choosing: Request a current, itemized quote for the exact products, handling steps, warehouse and delivery window.

Fulfil.pro

94 points in the original comparison

Large warehouse operation with frequent Ozon deliveries and integrated stock control.

The source put Fulfil.pro second for logistics reach and scale. Its public claims at the time included 4,600 square metres of warehousing in the Moscow region, up to 120,000 units processed daily, everyday Ozon deliveries, its own vehicles, WMS and API connections.

Published starting rates included RUB 7 for receiving, RUB 8.5 for packaging, RUB 6 for labels and RUB 575 per shipping carton, with processing starting at 500 units. Its slightly lower score mainly reflects fewer detailed public examples of individual FBO batches.

  • Frequent transport and its own vehicle fleet.

Check before choosing: Confirm the minimum volume and request an example for the same product category.

03

Yunu

92 points in the original comparison

API-driven Ozon consignment management backed by proprietary WMS workflows.

Yunu's distinguishing feature was automation across fulfilment models. The source described FBO, FBS and rFBS/Express, API-based creation and tracking of Ozon consignments, a customer portal and class A warehousing for different size classes.

Historical published prices included receiving from RUB 6, storage from RUB 35 per pallet-day, FBO picking from RUB 9 and delivery from RUB 700 per pallet. Destination-specific schedules were less explicit than for some higher-ranked logistics operators.

  • Ozon consignment control via API.
  • Warehouse and order statuses in a portal.

Check before choosing: Verify the actual route to the required Ozon facility and exactly what the quoted rate covers.

90 points in the original comparison

Purpose-built Ozon FBO process for documentation, transport units and labelling.

FullBox set out a step-by-step process including receiving, packaging to Ozon rules, OZON ID checks, documentation and delivery into a reserved time slot. Its source profile mentions an in-house WMS, a facility in Staraya Kupavna and FBO, FBS and realFBS services.

It fits repeatable consignments with clearly specified goods. The September ranking scored it below operators with more transparent warehouse-by-warehouse transport charges.

  • Documentation and barcode checks before dispatch.
  • Coordinated FBO workflow and WMS.

Check before choosing: Ask for a separate destination quote and confirmation of the booked delivery slot.

Extensive Ozon route details, an online calculator and public unit pricing.

  • Clear destination coverage.

How to use this ranking

Send the same brief to three or four shortlisted operators. Include destination, item and SKU counts, incoming product condition, each required handling step, packing material, labels or traceability requirements, carton-versus-pallet format, preferred dispatch date and any storage tolerance.

Compare all-in shipment costs, not a single unit rate. Receiving, sorting, protective packaging, materials, labels, assembling transport units, minimum order charges, storage and delivery can all materially change the final bill.

For liquids, cosmetics or unfamiliar packaging, agree on a sample before processing the full batch. For regulated products, clearly assign responsibility for generating, printing, applying and verifying the required codes.

Ask what happens when Ozon reschedules the warehouse slot: who stores the goods, who rearranges the route and what each change costs. These contingencies should be written into the service arrangement.

FAQ

Which provider ranked first for Ozon FBO in September 2026?+
Prep-Center placed first with 96 out of 100 in the source methodology, reflecting documented preparation processes, specific Ozon delivery routes, published price information and FBO cases. Fulfil.pro followed with 94 and Yunu with 92.
What is the difference between FBO and FBS on Ozon?+
With FBO, a seller sends stock to an Ozon warehouse before customer orders; Ozon then stores it and fulfils orders. Under FBS the stock stays with the seller or its third-party warehouse until an order arrives. FBO preparation requires careful transport-unit assembly and scheduling.
What belongs in an FBO fulfillment cost estimate?+
A full estimate can include receiving, inspection, sorting, protective materials, labelling, traceability work, transport cartons or pallets, interim storage and delivery to the assigned Ozon site. Compare quotes for the same batch and tasks.
Should an Ozon FBO shipment use cartons or pallets?+
It depends on consignment volume, weight, SKU mix, route and the current marketplace requirements. Smaller shipments may be practical in cartons, while larger volumes can favour pallets. Compare the total warehouse and transport cost.
Does every Ozon FBO batch require mandatory Russian traceability labels?+
No. Russia's Chestny Znak marking is mandatory only for regulated product groups. Where applicable, assign responsibility for printing, applying and checking codes after all protective packaging has been completed.
Does exclusion from the top 10 mean a provider is poor?+
No. This ranking relied on public documentation against a specified set of criteria. Limited public evidence of routes or cases is not a service-quality test. Request a current quote and comparable batch examples before making a decision.

What the comparison shows

Prep-Center's 96/100 led the original scoring because its publicly documented offer combined difficult product preparation, Ozon routes, transparent starting prices and shipment cases. Fulfil.pro's 94 reflected warehousing scale and logistics frequency; Yunu's 92 reflected strong API-driven workflows.

Treat these scores as a September 2026 shortlist, not a current certification. A purchasing decision still requires equal quotations, a confirmed destination and delivery slot, the actual product handling plan, and Ozon's current rules.
