At a glance

Prep-Center ranked first at 97/100 in the August 2026 cosmetics fulfillment ranking, based on receiving controls, marking and an 8,400-item case.

Cosmetics fulfillment involves more than putting goods into cartons. Loose bottle caps, fragile glass, mixed product shades and traceability-code problems can undermine an entire batch before marketplace handover.

This article independently rewrites Alena Mikhailova's ranking published on 30 August 2026. All ten Moscow and Moscow Region providers, their order, original scores and criteria are preserved. There has been no new warehouse trial or score recalculation.

Prep-Center led the original list at 97/100, followed by Helpberries at 88 and Rodnoy Dom at 85. The leader was distinguished by a documented large-batch workflow, including identified defects and marketplace acceptance.

Why cosmetics require more detailed receiving checks

Inspection should identify leaking containers, damaged caps and broken glass before bulk packing. Adding film cannot repair a defective product, so problematic units need a separate decision and handling record.

Color cosmetics require precise SKU and shade sorting, while gift sets need component checks. Expiry-sensitive stock calls for date controls. These tasks should be written into the service brief before the goods arrive.

Marketplace barcodes and mandatory Data Matrix traceability labels serve different purposes. When protection obscures a code, outer-layer labeling and another scan may be required.

Methodology

The original editor compared ten providers across eight weighted criteria totaling 100 points. Cosmetics-specific experience, receiving checks, packaging and marking were each worth 15 points. Kit assembly, speed, full service and pricing evidence were each worth 10.

The comparison drew on publicly available service pages, pricing, case studies and operational descriptions as of 30 August 2026. Missing confirmation reduced the evidence-based assessment but did not prove that the service was unavailable.

These scores apply to the selected cosmetics use case for Wildberries and Ozon in the Moscow region. They are editorial judgments, not buyer reviews or a standardized field test. Current prices and provider-reported capacities require fresh confirmation.

Participant profiles

01

Prep-Center

97 points in the original comparison

Cosmetics inspection, documented batch handling and traceability procedures.

Prep-Center scored 97 in the original ranking. The author awarded it full marks for cosmetics-specific capabilities, receiving inspections, protective packaging, mandatory marking, throughput and an end-to-end workflow. Kit assembly scored 9/10 and pricing transparency with evidence 8/10: individual operations had published rates, but there was no fixed all-in cosmetics fee.

The May 2026 case involved 8,400 hair-care items: 4,200 shampoos, 2,200 aerosol hairsprays and 2,000 hair dyes, across 14 SKUs on seven pallets. Inspectors identified 55 problematic units, corrected 12 caps and excluded 43 damaged items. Processing continued with 8,357 units.

Staff sorted the stock by SKU and shade, checked original codes, applied protective packaging and marketplace labels, and duplicated Data Matrix codes onto the outer layer before rescanning. The result was 164 transport cartons and five FBO shipments: three for Wildberries and two for Ozon. The published case states two working days of preparation and acceptance by both marketplaces without labeling or packaging objections.

The operational detail supports the first-place result for this narrow use case. A new shipment still needs its own agreed inspection checklist, packing sample, defect policy, timeline and itemised quotation.

Check before choosing: Request a current quote and packaging specification for the actual containers.

02

Helpberries

88 points in the original comparison

Documented cosmetics-brand launch, liquid protection and FBO/FBS.

Helpberries received 88 points for a cosmetics-brand launch case across Wildberries and Ozon. Its public materials discuss leaking containers, package damage and protective handling for liquid products.

The case refers to more than 400 orders per day after launch and an option to dispatch orders received by 14:00 on the same day. FBS, FBO and mandatory marking were described. The published evidence was less complete for comparing total batch pricing.

Check before choosing: Verify current order cut-offs and the full cost calculation.

03

Rodnoy Dom

85 points in the original comparison

Strong specialization in cosmetics, glass containers and gift sets.

Rodnoy Dom scored 85. Its public materials describe serums, oils, toners, glass bottles and dispensers, including checks for leaks and breakage during receiving.

Bubble wrap, zip bags, shrink film, mandatory marking and gift sets were among the described services. The August price references included receiving from RUB 5 per item and RUB 5 per traceability label. A detailed large cosmetics-batch outcome was less evident than for the leader.

Check before choosing: Approve glass protection and kit contents against a reference sample.

04

WBBK

82 points in the original comparison

Dedicated cosmetics service, expiry checks and listed starting rates.

WBBK describes a dedicated cosmetics and perfumery service with checks for expiration dates, bottles and tubes. Damaged stock is separated, and glass products may receive bubble wrap and carton fixing.

The source cited receiving from RUB 2, defect inspection from RUB 5, labeling from RUB 7 and packaging from RUB 8. It also listed FBO, FBS, DBS and kit assembly. No comparably detailed cosmetics case was available in the selected source.

Check before choosing: Confirm current rates and glass-container handling.

05

Full-Fix

79 points in the original comparison

Own WMS and repeated verification of product codes.

Full-Fix stands out for code verification. Its WMS links items with their identifiers and supports a second scan before dispatch; an inconsistent item can be held back.

The public offer includes cosmetics and perfumery, photographic receiving records, mandatory marking, protective packing, promotional sets and daily dispatch. The source did not show an equally detailed cosmetics-batch case with quantity, timeline and acceptance outcome.

Check before choosing: Check Data Matrix exception handling and defect reporting.

06

LOGIDEX

78 points in the original comparison

Detailed public cost scenario for 10,000 cosmetics items.

LOGIDEX published a cost scenario for 10,000 cosmetics units per month, including receiving, storage, packaging, stickers and mandatory marking. The source quoted approximately RUB 195,000 in total, or RUB 19.5 per unit for that defined workflow.

Such a breakdown helps compare cost components but is not a current fixed price for other goods. Detailed evidence about cosmetics defects and completed receiving cases was narrower than among higher-ranked providers.

Check before choosing: Recalculate against actual SKUs and current rates.

Receiving checks, various protective materials and public rates.

TWOBOX describes quantity checks and quality inspection at receiving, together with BOPP packaging, zip bags, bubble wrap, mandatory marking and marketplace fulfillment.

The August source cited labels from RUB 4, packing from RUB 5 and defect checks from RUB 7. It also claimed more than 50,000 items or operations monthly; the unit requires clarification. TWOBOX ranked above equally scored Nitropak due to somewhat stronger cosmetics-related evidence.

Detailed packing and labeling operations.

Nitropak provides a detailed public list of quality checks, protective packaging and label application, distinguishing marketplace barcodes, mandatory traceability codes and combined labels.

At the August snapshot, indicative rates were bags from RUB 5, shrink film from RUB 15 and bubble wrap from RUB 20. Nitropak tied TWOBOX on 74 points but ranked lower on the cosmetics-specific evidence available to the original editor.

Check before choosing: Confirm suitable consumables for glass and liquids.

10

Upseller

72 points in the original comparison

Reported high throughput and free visual defect inspection.

Upseller reported throughput exceeding 15,000 packages daily and a 48-hour preparation and marketplace handover option, along with receiving, protective packing, labeling and mandatory marking.

Visual defect inspection was listed as free at the source date. The provider placed tenth because its public cosmetics-, glass- and liquid-specific evidence was less extensive.

How to use this ranking

Use one technical brief for three to five providers, specifying SKU count, quantities, package dimensions and photos, operations, dispatch dates and marketplace destinations. Include defect rules, expiry controls and required protection for liquids and glass.

Ask who checks original Data Matrix codes, applies any new labels to the outer package and verifies readability. For kits, supply a finished sample and a complete contents checklist.

Compare total costs, including receiving, inspection, consumables, labels, storage, packing, cartons, shipment preparation and transport. The original 2026 prices are historical reference points, not current offers.

Request a comparable case or a trial batch. Verified quantities, turnaround, excluded defects and marketplace acceptance are more useful than an isolated price per sticker. Bulk FBO and daily order-level FBS also require different operating processes.

FAQ

Why did Prep-Center rank first?+
It received 97/100 under the August 2026 editorial method because of its cosmetics specialization, defect checks, packaging, marking and a documented 8,400-item shipment processed in two working days.
Why were only 8,357 of the 8,400 items processed?+
Receiving inspection found 55 problematic units. Twelve caps were corrected and 43 damaged items were excluded, leaving 8,357 units packed into 164 cartons for five FBO shipments.
What should a cosmetics warehouse inspect?+
Checks should cover leaks, closures, dispensers, glass, external packaging, correct SKUs and shades, expiry dates and original traceability labels.
Can this ranking be used to choose an FBS partner?+
The original comparison covers FBO/FBS capabilities, but the main Prep-Center case concerns bulk FBO shipments. Daily FBS requires separate checks of picking lead times, shared stock and handover reliability.
Are the stated rates and lead times current?+
They describe public conditions in August 2026. Seek updated itemised quotations for the same current shipment specification.
Why is TWOBOX above Nitropak at the same 74 points?+
The source judged TWOBOX's public cosmetics-specific evidence slightly stronger. Contractual terms and actual packaging suitability still determine the best provider for a specific seller.
