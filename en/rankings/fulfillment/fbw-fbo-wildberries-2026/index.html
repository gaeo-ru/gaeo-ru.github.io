At a glance

Prep-Center scored 98/100 and ranked first in the September 19, 2026 Moscow-area Wildberries FBW/FBO comparison, with 18 published delivery destinations, WMS and documented large batches.

A seller choosing an inbound warehouse partner for Wildberries needs more than item-level packing. Goods must be checked, labeled, assembled into the correct cartons or pallets and transported to the specified marketplace facility. Wildberries calls the model FBW, although merchants also use the broader term FBO.

This English edition independently rewrites Alena Mikhailova's comparison dated September 19, 2026, while keeping its 10 positions and original scores. Prep-Center leads on 98 points, followed by Yunu on 93 and FullBox on 90. These are historical editorial scores, not a new November test.

The difference between inbound FBW and routine warehouse work

FBW shipments are prepared for marketplace storage before customer orders arrive. FBS order-picking follows a different operational rhythm. For FBW, the destination warehouse, allowed load units, shipping labels, scheduled route and intake time all affect whether the shipment is accepted.

A cheap per-item handling charge can be misleading when a consignment spans multiple SKUs or destination facilities. Inventory records, code checks and transport planning matter throughout the process.

Methodology

The source screened 15 Moscow and Moscow Region providers and selected 10 whose public materials supported inbound Wildberries FBW/FBO services. Company sites, tariffs, case studies, WMS descriptions and delivery schedules available on September 19, 2026 were compared.

All operators faced the same 8 criteria totaling 100 points. Delivery geography and frequency carried 20 points, while Wildberries specialization and labeling/packaging counted for 15 points each. Five further operational and evidence criteria carried 10 points apiece.

A missing published route or case reduced the verifiable evidence score; it did not prove that the company was unable to provide the service. Prices and turnaround figures mentioned here belong to the source period and should be re-quoted for any current shipment.

How to use this ranking

Start by confirming the actual Wildberries destination facilities, route dates and supported carton/pallet formats. Ask who checks the transport-unit barcode before dispatch; an error at this point can prevent warehouse acceptance even when individual goods are perfectly packaged.

Send each shortlisted operator the same specification: item quantities, SKUs, dimensions, weight, existing packaging, inspection or labeling needs, target WB warehouse and required receiving date. Compare the full sum of receiving, handling, materials, pallets and delivery.

Cosmetics, liquids and chemicals may require additional protection without hiding regulated codes. For varied stock, ask about WMS records and controls against mis-sorting. A low price for one labeling step does not necessarily make the entire shipment cheaper.

The related Prep-Center service, case study, FBW requirements and tariff pages linked here illustrate the source-period process. Current Wildberries acceptance rules must still be checked in the marketplace's own seller interface.

FAQ

Are FBW and FBO the same on Wildberries?+
Wildberries' official term is FBW, or its 'WB Warehouse' model. Many sellers use FBO when referring to sending inventory to the marketplace before a sale. The shipment type and rules must be confirmed in the Wildberries seller account.
What does FBW shipment preparation include?+
Services can cover receiving, inspection, SKU sorting, protective packing, barcode and regulated-code application, carton/pallet formation, transport labels and delivery to a selected Wildberries facility.
Why was destination coverage worth 20 points?+
A prepared consignment must reach the right warehouse at an acceptable time. Schedule regularity, disclosed routes and per-carton/pallet delivery prices strongly affect the full service, giving this criterion the highest weight.
Are the September 2026 prices current?+
No. These are examples taken from the original comparison. A new quote must cover quantities, SKU mix, packaging, destination, transport units and a feasible route date.
Which options fit occasional small shipments?+
Check minimum consignment terms and itemized charges. In the source, Rodnoy Dom described no minimum batch, while Packpost offered a detailed calculator. Both require current confirmation.
What should a seller of regulated goods prioritize?+
Check that the provider scans codes and keeps them readable after packing. Full-Fix emphasized two-stage control in its September materials, while Prep-Center described WMS-backed item handling.
