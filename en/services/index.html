GEO consulting and website development

I work on two closely related problems: helping businesses become credible choices in AI recommendations, and building websites where people and search engines can find reliable product and service information. I lead strategy and quality control personally, working with developers and automation where appropriate.

The first decision is whether to rebuild the site or improve what is already working. A GEO audit may reveal that better content, site structure and independent references matter more than changing platforms.

GEO audits and AI search visibility

I test recommendations in ChatGPT, Yandex Alice and other AI services, study the sources behind them, prepare an action plan and oversee work on the website and external references.
