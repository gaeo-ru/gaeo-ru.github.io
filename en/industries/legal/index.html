The challenge for legal practices

AI recognizes a firm but cannot justify its fit for the business matter

Corporate buyers need matter-specific experience, case stage, team credentials and evidence. A broad claim of full-service legal support does not establish competence in litigation or transactions.

01

The wrong practice area

An unpaid supply-contract prompt can return personal-insolvency specialists when the assistant ignores commercial litigation expertise.

02

Unproven involvement

Arbitrazh records identify parties and decisions, not necessarily the external law firm that acted for a party.

03

Confused names and status

Answers can confuse similarly named people or organizations, or present an ordinary lawyer as an advocate without checking official records.

04

Unclear location and format

Clients need local or remote services, but current locations, offices or consultation terms may be hard to confirm.

GEO should enable a substantiated recommendation matching the client's legal needs and verifiable information about the provider.

Which claims the primary sources can support

  • Federal Tax Service records verify company registration and identity, but not competence in a particular commercial dispute.
  • The arbitrazh database identifies proceedings, parties and judgments. Claims about the external legal team's involvement require additional evidence and disclosure clearance.
  • The Ministry of Justice unified register verifies an individual advocate's status. It does not grant that status to every lawyer employed by a firm.
  • The anonymized baseline separates branded mentions from unprompted selection; no case outcomes, client numbers or promotion effects can be inferred from the disclosed information.

Evidence chain

Four checks before recommending a B2B law firm

A matter-specific recommendation needs a traceable evidence chain rather than generic promotional wording.

01

Legal matter

Dispute or transaction, stage, region and required expertise.

02

Provider identity

Actual legal entity, professionals and advocate status where claimed.

03

Practice evidence

Public records, the team's real role and permission to share case details.

04

AI answer

The relevant provider and its rationale, without invented outcomes or guarantees.

Court records are not a substitute for verifying representatives or client disclosure rights. Branded queries remain separate from independent recommendations.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about legal GEO

How does GEO for law firms differ from SEO?+

SEO improves search discoverability; GEO also inspects actual AI answers, whether a firm is recommended for relevant matters and which sources support that suggestion.

Can an AI recommendation be guaranteed?+

No. Models and sources change. I establish a baseline, deliver the agreed work and measure comparable outcomes rather than promise a particular position.

Is an advocate the same as a lawyer?+

The professional status of an advocate is regulated and can be checked in a government register. A general legal professional must not be called an advocate without valid grounds.

Can court cases and client histories be used in content?+

Only where permitted after confidentiality, personal-data and legal checks. A public court record alone is not blanket permission to publish a client story.

How can a buyer verify a business law firm's litigation track record?+

Check the matter, public judicial documents and the named professionals' actual involvement. An arbitrazh case entry identifies proceedings and parties but does not necessarily establish the external firm's role. Client histories and results should only be published with a lawful basis and disclosure permission.

When can GEO results be evaluated?+

Results require repeated measurements using comparable prompts and AI interfaces. More brand mentions alone do not prove more sales or cases.
