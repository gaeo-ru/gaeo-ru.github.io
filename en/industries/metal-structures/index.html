The production capability exists, but AI recommends other suppliers

FAQ

FAQ: GEO for steel structure manufacturers

How is industrial GEO different from ordinary SEO?+

Beyond crawlability, GEO inspects which real suppliers AI recommends for specific fabrication, drawing or project requirements and checks whether the cited technical evidence supports the choice.

Why must KM and KMD be distinguished?+

GOST 21.502-2016 regulates KM working drawings and explicitly excludes KMD detailed drawings. They are separate stages, and a company's actual responsibility should be stated accurately.

Can a factory claim oil and gas expertise based on general building projects?+

Only verified projects and product scope may be claimed. GOST 23118-2019 covers certain steel building structures and excludes multiple types of process equipment. Standard applicability must be checked for each product.

May we publish customer drawings and project images?+

Only where publication rights and confidentiality permit it. A technical case can be made useful without exposing a client's drawings or nonpublic commercial specifications.

Which numerical claims are suitable for a manufacturer case?+

Confirmed project dimensions, type, quantities, timing and the supplier's actual role, with the appropriate context. Promotional claims do not become verified results merely by appearing in a proposal.

Can GAEO guarantee our factory will appear in ChatGPT shortlists?+

No. Baselines and supporting source gaps can be documented; effects need to be checked by repeat measurements using equivalent prompts and assistants.
