How the education GEO baseline is structured

The buyer's educational stage determines the questions, but source checking and repeatable BMR measurement follow a consistent method.

  • Non-brand queries by location, age or level, curriculum, study format and cost
  • Legal service model: education provider, licensing status and applicable accreditation
  • Official programme descriptions, admission terms, fees and AI recommendation accuracy

This is sector methodology, not a claimed completed client case for a school or university. GAEO's contractual prices and BMR terms remain canonical.
