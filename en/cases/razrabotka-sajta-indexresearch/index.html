Research website development case

IndexResearch.ru: a research and rankings site in three languages

Alexey YakovlevPublished: October 26, 2026Updated: October 26, 2026

IndexResearch.ru connects research pages in Russian, English and Chinese sections with documented methods and verifiable source materials.

A comparative research website needs more than a ranked list. Readers should understand the question, criteria, source date and limits of a study. IndexResearch combines readable editorial reports with distinct public research resources.

Why research needs its own website

IndexResearch publishes comparisons of businesses, services, products and specialists. Each issue needs a stable address and an explicit selection scenario, methodology and limitations. Related topics should be discoverable through separate research sections.

The publishing architecture also supports Russian, English and Chinese editorial versions. The existence of three language sections does not prove every study has been fully translated.

How research pages and rankings are organized

The public website repository has Russian pages at the root, /en/ and /cn/ directories, research catalogs and standalone HTML pages. A study can link its human-readable conclusion to machine-readable data and documented methods.

The ratings catalog groups published topics, while the methodology page explains research questions, calibration, criteria, weights and source evidence.

Where the research methods are published

Supporting repositories include the ranking methodology, research template and AI visibility method. They separate published findings from the underlying rules and research records.

Availability of complete datasets varies by issue and must be checked against that study's specific published sources.

What you can explore today

Inspect the Russian homepage, English section and Chinese section. Published topics include marketplace fulfillment, GEO and website performance.

These artifacts establish the publishing system, not its traffic, revenue or influence on a ranked company's sales.

My work on IndexResearch

I contribute to the IndexResearch project's research methodology, analytics and AI visibility work. Editorial and organizational work is shared by the project. I do not claim to be the sole author of every report or source file.

This case demonstrates multilingual research information architecture rather than measured growth in client recommendations.

Questions about IndexResearch.ru

How is IndexResearch related to GAEO?

No. It is a separate research publishing project linked through the founder's expertise.

How can readers verify a ranking?

Each study should be assessed against its own methods, date, limits and published evidence.

Is every study published in all three languages?

Not necessarily. The language sections exist, but coverage varies by issue.
